## THE SETTINGS, AND WHY THEY MATTER MORE THAN YOUR GUT

TEN TEAMS. SIXTEEN ROSTER SPOTS. PPR — ONE POINT PER CATCH. Redraft, snake, fourteen weeks of regular season, and SIX of ten teams make the playoffs. SIX OF TEN!

Do you understand what that does to a draft? It means the floor is a playoff team. Six of ten. You could half-ass this thing and still be alive in December, which is exactly why half this conference is going to draft like there's no consequence — and that is horseshit thinking, because sixteen spots across ten teams means the pool never runs dry and there is NO excuse for panicking in round three.

Two demands. One: every manager in this league should be BANNED from drafting a kicker before the last two rounds — sixteen spots is a mansion, not a studio apartment. Two: I want the six-team playoff field written on the wall of every draft room in caps so nobody pretends this was hard.
