# Gravel Pit Grinders Survive by Five, Stay Perfect at 7-0

## Seven and Oh. Say It Louder.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 and the scoreboard doesn't care how it looked. 134.2 to 128.9. Five point three points, in a game the paper called for by 121.6 to 118.3, and the paper missed both numbers. Week 7 in the Ironclad Fantasy Conference gave us a nail-biter at the top, a 45.2-point beatdown in the two-hole, and an upset that torched a projection sheet down in the middle of the seed order.

Six teams make the tournament. Right now that's the Grinders, the Cedar Falls Cormorants, the Quarry Road Quakers, the Ninth Street Nightjars, the Halyard Bay Harriers and the Foundry District Foxes. Everybody else is playing for a seat. And the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7, which is a hell of a thing to be after scoring 128.9.

## Game of the Week: 134.2-128.9, and the Bench Told the Whole Story

Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Five point three. Seven wins in a row, and the Grinders have not lost a game all season. SCOREBOARD!

Rex Dolan, 31.8 for the Grinders, projected 22.4. I don't care where he was taken. He was in the lineup and he went and got 31.8 in a game decided by 5.3. GIVE THAT MAN HIS FLOWERS.

Now the other sideline, because this is where the week actually got decided. Sable Ridge started Cole Vandermeer, and Cole Vandermeer scored 6.2. Duke Ellery sat on the bench and scored 24.1. Swap those two and the Sentinels pick up 17.9 — in a game they lost by 5.3. Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels left 78.6 on that bench, more than half of what her starters scored, and she still came within five of the best team in the league. That's the whole story right there.

Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels went on the record twice about the Duke Ellery call, and I'll give her this — she didn't dodge the number.
