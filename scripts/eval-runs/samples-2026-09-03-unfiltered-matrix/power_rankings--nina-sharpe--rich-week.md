# Week 7 Power Rankings: The Grinders Are 7-0 and the Sentinels Are 0-7 by Five Points

## Class, Take Your Seats

Class. Seven games. Say it with me: seven. That is not a season, that is a syllabus.

At the top of the board, Gravel Pit Grinders, 7-0, 812.4 points for, first in the league in both columns. Circle that. Cedar Falls Cormorants posted the week's high score, 141.6, and sit second at 788.9. Halyard Bay Harriers won the week's only listed upset, 127.5 to 103.8 over Ninth Street Nightjars.

And at the bottom, the row I will be teaching from: Sable Ridge Sentinels, 0-7, and this week they lost by 5.3 while their bench scored 78.6. Was it the schedule? It was not.

That is the week. Now the rows.

## The Board, Top to Bottom

**1. Gravel Pit Grinders (7-0, 812.4).** Projected 121.6, scored 134.2. Most points for in the league, best record in the league, same team. Dana Whitlock is not being carried by the schedule; the schedule is being carried. Rex Dolan projected 22.4 and scored 31.8. That is a real column. I checked it twice.

**2. Cedar Falls Cormorants (5-2, 788.9).** High score of the week at 141.6, projected 124.9, and a 45.2-point beating of Ashgrove Anvils. Cass Lindqvist: projected 17.6, scored 33.4. In week four I told this class Cedar Falls finishes top two in points for. They are second at 788.9. I am not going to pretend that was luck.

**3. Quarry Road Quakers (5-2, 761.3).** Won by 6.4 against a Milltown side that outscored its own projection in exactly no place that mattered. Nico Barrantes, projected 10.4, scored 19.7 — the tight end being the difference in a six-point game is not a strategy, it is a Tuesday. Priya Nandi also owns the only trade on the books, from week six: Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick in, Hank Osterberg out. Grade: incomplete. Two weeks is not a sample, it is an anecdote with a decimal.

**4. Ninth Street Nightjars (4-3, 744.8).** Pop quiz: which row here is the manager's? Projected 126.2, scored 103.8 — that is 22.4 under, and the bench scored 61.5. Tobias Reneau was projected 19.3 and scored 8.1. Trevor Ashby will get some sympathy from the results desk. He will get none from this column.

**5. Halyard Bay Harriers (4-3, 733.5).** Projected 109.4, scored 127.5, won by 23.7. Lowest bench points of the week at 33.8, which is what a correctly set lineup looks like from behind. Gil Provost, projected 20.7, scored 29.9. Lena Okafor gets full marks for the week and a reminder that 733.5 is still fifth in scoring.

**6. Foundry District Foxes (3-4, 720.7).** Beat Pike County by 21.8 behind Dane Kowalczyk, projected 16.8, scored 27.2. Sam Kestrel's team is 3-4 with 720.7 — sixth in scoring, sixth in the standings. The story wants a collapse. The column wants nothing. Row's fine.

**7. Milltown Mudlarks (3-4, 705.2).** Femi Adeyemi scored 26.3 on an 18.1 projection and the team still lost by 6.4, because the bench held 47.2. Owen Radcliffe spent 34 on Rudy Halvorsen Wednesday morning. Milltown didn't comment, so this is the box score's version, which is the version I'd have used anyway.

**8. Ashgrove Anvils (2-5, 688.6).** Projected 108.2, scored 96.4, lost by 45.2, streak L3. Bo Tremaine: projected 16.2, scored 4.8. Ashgrove declined to comment, which I am recording as a fact and not as a motive.

**9. Pike County Pilots (2-5, 664.1).** Ninth in scoring at 664.1, and the bench scored 55.3 against 99.6 from the starters. Ellis Marchetti, projected 15.4, scored 12.5. Hollis Vance shuffled a roster spot Wednesday. It did not move this column.

**10. Sable Ridge Sentinels (0-7, 651.9).** Last in scoring at 651.9 and — everyone look at the board — lowest points against in the league at 677.2. So the schedule has been the kindest thing in Ruth Tanaka's life and the record is 0-7. Duke Ellery was benched, would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, and that swap was worth 17.9 points in a game lost by 5.3. That is, and I am using the technical term, a fucking problem. Moving on.

## Who Moved, and Why the Column Says So

Riser: Halyard Bay Harriers. Projected 109.4, scored 127.5, benched 33.8 — the fewest bench points of any team this week. That is not variance, that is a lineup.

Faller: Ninth Street Nightjars. Highest projection of the week at 126.2, scored 103.8, benched 61.5, streak L1. One week. Hold it loosely. I am holding it loosely.

Honorable mention to Ashgrove Anvils, who are 2-5 with 758.3 points against — second-worst schedule luck on the board behind Pike County's 771.4. Partial credit for the schedule there. None for a 96.4.
