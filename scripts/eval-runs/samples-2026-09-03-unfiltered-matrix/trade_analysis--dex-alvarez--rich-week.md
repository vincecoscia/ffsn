# One Trade On The Books: Quarry Road Buys Bodies, Foundry District Buys A Back

## Here's what I've got

REPORTED: Quarry Road Quakers to Foundry District Foxes. Filed Thursday, Oct. 9, half past two.

Quarry Road gave Hank Osterberg. Quarry Road received Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick.

Two-for-one. No picks. No cash.

OPINION: it is also the entire trade market. One deal on the books through seven weeks in a ten-team league. Bleak.

## Quarry Road's side of the paper

REPORTED: the Quakers sit 5-2, third, 761.3 points for.

They took the two-for-one. Wide receiver and tight end in, running back out.

REPORTED, Week 7: Quarry Road 118.7, Milltown 112.3. Margin 6.4. Nico Barrantes went for 19.7 on a 10.4 projection. That is the tight end room doing work without any help from this trade.

STATED: nothing. Priya Nandi did not go on the record about the deal, and I am not going to build a motive out of an empty page.

My read, not reporting: a 5-2 team that trades one starter for two does it because it does not trust its bye weeks. Two names fill two holes. One name fills one. That is the whole logic, and at third place with a Week 8 home date against Ninth Street, it does not have to be cleverer than that.

Grade on file: B-plus. I did not set it. I do not hate it.

## Foundry District's side of the paper

REPORTED: the Foxes are 3-4, sixth, 720.7 points for, coming off a Week 7 win — Foundry District 121.4, Pike County 99.6.

REPORTED: they gave two, got one back. Osterberg joins a room that already had Dane Kowalczyk, who put up 27.2 on a 16.8 projection in that Week 7 win.

STATED: nothing. Sam Kestrel did not go on the record. Only one thing was asked and no answer came back on either side of this trade, so this section is short on purpose.

Consolidation is a real strategy. Trading two starters for one is also how a 3-4 team ends up with a thin bench in November. Grade on file: B-minus.
