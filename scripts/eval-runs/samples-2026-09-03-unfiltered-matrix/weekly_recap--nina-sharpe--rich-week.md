# Seven Columns, One Bench: Gravel Pit Grinders Survive by 5.3

## Class, Take Your Seats

Class. Week 7 of fourteen, six playoff spots, and the top of the board did not move. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points, first in both columns, which is rarer than the results desk downstairs will tell you. Circle the row underneath: Cedar Falls Cormorants 5-2 at 788.9, Quarry Road Quakers 5-2 at 761.3. Three teams, two games of separation, seven of fourteen weeks gone.

At the bottom, Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 with 651.9 points, last in both. And still — this is the fun part — they lost this week by 5.3.

Seven games is a sample you hold loosely. I'm holding it loosely. I am also still right about most of it.

## Game of the Week: 5.3 Points and a Bench

Everyone look at the board. Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9, margin 5.3. The 7-0 team beat the 0-7 team by less than one good catch, and the interesting column is not the one with the winner in it.

Start with the honest part. The Grinders were projected 121.6 and scored 134.2 — 12.6 over. Rex Dolan: projected 22.4, scored 31.8. That is a quarterback beating his number by 9.4 in a game decided by 5.3. Dana Whitlock's team won this on the field, and I will say so plainly.

Now the Sentinels. Projected 118.3, scored 128.9, so 10.6 over their own number as well. Ivan Petrosky went 28.6 on a projected 14.9. Both teams outscored the projection; only one of them left the week with a win. Pop quiz: which row explains that?

Not the projection. Not the schedule. Circle the bench. Sable Ridge's bench scored 78.6. The Grinders' bench scored 41.3 — subtract, and that is 37.3 more points sitting still on the losing side. Inside that 78.6 is Duke Ellery, projected 11.7, scored 24.1, sitting behind Cole Vandermeer, projected 15.8, scored 6.2. Swap those two and Sable Ridge gains 17.9. They lost by 5.3.

The story says the Sentinels are 0-7 because they are the worst team in the league. They are last in points at 651.9, so the story gets partial credit. The column says this particular loss was one lineup slot, and 17.9 is more than three times the margin. Seven games. Hold it loosely. I am holding it loosely with both hands and it still says bench.

Season claim from Week 5, still open: Sable Ridge does not win before Thanksgiving. Nothing here moved it.

## The Other Four Rows

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Top score of the week and a 45.2-point margin. Cass Lindqvist: projected 17.6, scored 33.4, an overage of 15.8 all by himself. Ashgrove was projected 108.2 and scored 96.4, and Bo Tremaine turned a projected 16.2 into 4.8. Three straight losses now, 2-5. Bea Coleridge declined to comment, so this is the box score's version, which is the version I'd have used anyway. Also on the board: back in Week 4 I said Cedar Falls finishes top two in points for. They are second at 788.9. Filed, cashed, moving on.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** The most honest game of the week and the closest thing to a coin flip. Milltown was the higher projection at 119.8 to 114.5 and lost by 6.4. Femi Adeyemi went 26.3 against a projected 18.1 and it did not matter, because Nico Barrantes turned a projected 10.4 into 19.7 at tight end — a 9.3-point overage from the position where nobody plans for one. Milltown also spent 34 on Rudy Halvorsen at the Wednesday waiver run; I don't have a snap count to test that with, so I'm not going to pretend to grade it. Milltown didn't respond to the comment request.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The week's only upset, and it is not close to ambiguous. Ninth Street was projected 126.2 and scored 103.8 — a 22.4-point shortfall. Halyard Bay was projected 109.4 and scored 127.5, an 18.1-point overage. Gil Provost: projected 20.7, scored 29.9. Tobias Reneau, starting for the Nightjars: projected 19.3, scored 8.1.

And then the row Trevor Ashby should sit with. The Nightjars bench scored 61.5. The starters scored 103.8. No individual bench line carries a flag I can hang on a name, so I won't invent one — but 61.5 sitting behind a 22.4-point miss is, and I am using the technical term, a fucking problem. Moving on.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk: projected 16.8, scored 27.2. Pike County was projected 107.9, scored 99.6, and Ellis Marchetti's 12.5 was their headline number — that tells you enough. Foxes to 3-4 and the sixth seed by 15.5 points of points-for over Milltown, 720.7 to 705.2. That is the whole playoff cutline right now: fifteen and a half points.

## Studs, Duds, and One Bench Slip

Studs, in order of how much they beat their own projection:

Cass Lindqvist, Cedar Falls Cormorants — 33.4 on a projected 17.6. Ivan Petrosky, Sable Ridge Sentinels — 28.6 on a projected 14.9, and the best 28.6 nobody got to enjoy. Rex Dolan, Gravel Pit Grinders — 31.8 on a projected 22.4, highest raw score by a starter this week bar Lindqvist. Dane Kowalczyk, Foundry District Foxes, 27.2 on 16.8. Gil Provost, Halyard Bay Harriers, 29.9 on 20.7. Nico Barrantes, Quarry Road Quakers, 19.7 on 10.4 — smallest raw number on this list, and it decided a 6.4-point game.

Duds, same test:

Bo Tremaine, Ashgrove Anvils — 4.8 on a projected 16.2. Tobias Reneau, Ninth Street Nightjars — 8.1 on a projected 19.3. Cole Vandermeer, Sable Ridge Sentinels — 6.2 on a projected 15.8, and he was the one Duke Ellery's 24.1 would have replaced.

That is one clean column: every dud this week was a starter who missed by double digits. I checked it twice. I may frame it.
