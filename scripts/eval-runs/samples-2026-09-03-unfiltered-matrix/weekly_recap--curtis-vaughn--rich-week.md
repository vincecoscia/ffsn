# Gravel Pit Grinders Survive at Seven-and-Oh

## Good Evening

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in this league beat the only winless one by 5.3 points, and that is the whole week in one line.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7. The gap between them on Sunday was 134.2 to 128.9, which is the closest thing this league has produced to a plot twist.

Elsewhere: the Cedar Falls Cormorants put up the top score of the week at 141.6, and the Halyard Bay Harriers won a game they were projected to lose by 16.8 — 126.2 to 109.4 on the projection, 103.8 to 127.5 on the board.

Six playoff spots. Seven weeks gone, seven to play. Let's go to the board.

## Game of the Week: Five Points at the Top and the Bottom

Tightest game on the board: the Gravel Pit Grinders over the Sable Ridge Sentinels, 134.2 to 128.9. Margin, 5.3.

Rex Dolan led all scorers in the game for the Grinders with 31.8 points against a 22.4 projection. Dana Whitlock's team has now won seven straight and sits at 812.4 points for, the most in the league. The waiver discipline that got them here has held all season, and this week the number did the complimenting.

On the other side, Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 for the Sentinels on a 14.9 projection. Cole Vandermeer started and returned 6.2 against a 15.8 projection.

And this is the item. Duke Ellery was on the Sable Ridge bench and scored 24.1 points. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer in the lineup, for a gain of 17.9 points. The final margin was 5.3.

Sable Ridge Sentinels bench production: 78.6. Gravel Pit Grinders bench production: 41.3. Do with that what you will.

Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels did go on the record about the bench call, and we have that later in the show. Nina has the bench math, and I suspect she has more of it than I do.

For those keeping score at home: seven weeks, seven losses, and the closest of them came against the best team in the league. Stay with us.

## Around the Board

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Highest score of the week, and a 45.2-point margin. Cass Lindqvist did the heavy work for the Cormorants with 33.4 points on a 17.6 projection. For the Anvils, Bo Tremaine started and finished with 4.8 against a 16.2 projection. Ashgrove is 2-5 and has now lost three straight. Marcus Bly's team also spent 12 on Trey Ballinger at the wire on Wednesday morning. We reached out to the Ashgrove Anvils and Bea Coleridge declined to comment. Noted.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Second-closest game of the week at 6.4, and the Mudlarks were the projected favorite — 119.8 to 114.5. Nico Barrantes went for 19.7 at tight end for the Quakers on a 10.4 projection. Femi Adeyemi was the best player on the field with 26.3 for Milltown and it did not matter. Owen Radcliffe's team also spent 34 at the wire Wednesday morning to claim Rudy Halvorsen, dropping Pete Sandoval. We reached out to the Milltown Mudlarks and did not hear back. Noted. Dex is working the phones on that backfield.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The only upset on the board. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and delivered 103.8, a shortfall of 22.4. Gil Provost put up 29.9 for the Harriers on a 20.7 projection. Tobias Reneau started for the Nightjars and returned 8.1 against a 19.3 projection. Trevor Ashby's team left 61.5 points on the bench and lost by 23.7. We'll leave that there.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk carried the Foxes with 27.2 on a 16.8 projection. Ellis Marchetti led Pike County with 12.5, which was under his 15.4 projection. The Pilots are 2-5 on a two-game slide and put 55.3 on their bench. Sam Kestrel's team is 3-4 and holds the sixth and final playoff seed.

One trade on the books this season, from Thursday afternoon, Oct. 9: the Quarry Road Quakers sent Hank Osterberg to the Foundry District Foxes for Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick. Both teams won this week. Dex has the aftermath.

## Studs and Duds

Let's go to the board one more time.

Top line of the week: Cass Lindqvist, 33.4 points for the Cedar Falls Cormorants against a 17.6 projection. That is a score.

Behind him, Rex Dolan at 31.8 for the Gravel Pit Grinders on a 22.4 projection, and Gil Provost at 29.9 for the Halyard Bay Harriers on a 20.7 projection. Two quarterbacks, two wins.

Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 for the Sable Ridge Sentinels on a 14.9 projection and the team lost by 5.3. We'll leave that there.

Now the other end. Bo Tremaine started for the Ashgrove Anvils and returned 4.8 against a 16.2 projection, in a game his team lost by 45.2. Tobias Reneau started for the Ninth Street Nightjars and returned 8.1 against a 19.3 projection, in a game his team lost by 23.7. Cole Vandermeer started for Sable Ridge and returned 6.2 against a 15.8 projection, and the man who would have replaced him scored 24.1 from the bench.

Why any of them came in that far under, we don't have; nobody at this desk can tell you more than the final line.

Nina has the bench math on all three, and there is plenty of it. Coming up: the managers who spoke.
