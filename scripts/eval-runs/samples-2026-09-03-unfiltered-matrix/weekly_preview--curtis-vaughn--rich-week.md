# Week 8 Preview: Unbeaten Gravel Pit Grinders Draw Cedar Falls at the Top of the Board

## Good Evening. Here's the Running Order.

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in this league draws the second-highest scoring team in this league, and everything else on the week 8 board is a middle-class squabble.

Let's go to the board. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points for, the most in the league. The Cedar Falls Cormorants are 5-2 with 788.9, second. They meet in game one.

Behind them: the Quarry Road Quakers at 5-2, the Ninth Street Nightjars and Halyard Bay Harriers both 4-3, the Foundry District Foxes and Milltown Mudlarks both 3-4. Six teams make the playoffs. Half this league is auditioning.

At the bottom, the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 on a seven-game losing streak and travel to the 2-5 Pike County Pilots. Nina has the bench math from last week, and there is a lot of it.

Coming up: the marquee, the rest of the slate, and three managers who went on the record. Stay with us.

## The Marquee: Grinders vs. Cormorants

Two teams, 1,601.3 combined points for — 812.4 plus 788.9 — and one head-to-head meeting already on the books, which the Gravel Pit Grinders won 1-0 in the series. That is the whole history.

ESPN's projection, and it is a projection and not a score, has it 124.8 to 122.1 in favor of the Grinders. Two and seven tenths. On a board where last week produced a 45.2-point blowout, that is the closest projected game of the week.

Dana Whitlock's Grinders come in off a 134.2-128.9 win over Sable Ridge — five and change, with 41.3 points sitting on their own bench. Marcus Bly's Cormorants come in off 141.6, the highest single-team number posted last week, with 52.7 on the bench behind it. Both of these teams are winning while leaving a starter's worth of production in the garage. Nina has the bench math; I'm just reading the totals.

Cedar Falls has also been busy on the wire, adding Trey Ballinger on a waiver claim Wednesday morning. Dex is working the phones on whether that roster is done moving.

The case for the Grinders is that they have not lost, and the case for the Cormorants is that nobody has scored more than they did last week. I'll take the unbeaten team at home and expect the smallest margin of the week. We'll have more on that after the break.

## The Rest of the Board

Quarry Road Quakers vs. Ninth Street Nightjars. Priya Nandi's Quakers are 5-2 with 761.3 points for and just took a 6.4-point decision from Milltown. Trevor Ashby's Nightjars are 4-3 with 744.8 and lost 103.8-127.5 last week with 61.5 points on their bench, the second-largest bench figure on the board. The projection favors the visitors 119.4 to 116.9. That is the only game this week where the projection likes the road team with the worse record. Do with that what you will.

Halyard Bay Harriers vs. Foundry District Foxes. Lena Okafor's Harriers are 4-3 and riding a two-game winning streak. Sam Kestrel's Foxes are 3-4 and coming off a 121.4-99.6 win. Projected 117.6 to 113.2, Foxes. Both teams are chasing the sixth seed, which the Foxes currently hold. This one is worth your attention.

Milltown Mudlarks vs. Ashgrove Anvils. The Mudlarks are 3-4, the Anvils 2-5 on a three-game skid, and the Anvils lead the series 1-0. Projected 110.5 to 104.3. Milltown put in a waiver claim for Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning at 34 dollars. We reached out to the Milltown Mudlarks and did not hear back. Noted. The Ashgrove Anvils declined to comment.

Pike County Pilots vs. Sable Ridge Sentinels. The Pilots are 2-5 with 664.1 points for, the Sentinels 0-7 with 651.9, the two lowest totals in the league. The projection likes Sable Ridge 112.9 to 108.7, which is not something the standings would tell you. Back in week 5 this desk said Sable Ridge does not win a game before Thanksgiving. That one is still open, and I am not moving off it. Reggie will tell you the scoreboard is all that matters, and he's up next.

## Two Names Worth Your Attention

Short segment, thin wire. The free-agent list didn't come through this week, so we work from rosters.

Ivan Petrosky of the Sable Ridge Sentinels put up 28.6 last week against a projection of 14.9 — nearly fourteen points of overage on a team that has not won. He goes to Pike County as the one reliable thing on that roster.

Nico Barrantes of the Quarry Road Quakers scored 19.7 against a 10.4 projection at tight end. In a game projected inside three points, that is the position that decides it.

That's all we have on it right now. Nina will have more when the numbers settle.
