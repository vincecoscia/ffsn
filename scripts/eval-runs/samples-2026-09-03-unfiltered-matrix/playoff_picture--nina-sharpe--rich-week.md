# Seven Games In, The Ironclad Playoff Board Has Two Real Gaps

## Class, Circle The Top Six

Class. Seven games played, fourteen on the schedule, six playoff spots. Hold everything below loosely. I am holding it loosely. I am also still right.

The board, top to bottom in the six: the Gravel Pit Grinders at 7-0 and 812.4 points, first seed and the only unbeaten row on it. The Cedar Falls Cormorants, 5-2, 788.9, winners of two straight. The Quarry Road Quakers, 5-2, 761.3. Then the Ninth Street Nightjars, 4-3, 744.8, coming off a loss. The Halyard Bay Harriers, 4-3, 733.5, winners of two straight. And the sixth seed, the Foundry District Foxes, 3-4, 720.7.

Circle the second and third rows, because they are the same record — 5-2 and 5-2 — and the standings separate them by points: 788.9 to 761.3, a gap of 27.6. Same story at four and five: two 4-3 teams, 744.8 against 733.5, 11.3 apart. Nobody has told me how this league breaks a tie at the end, so I am not going to guess at it on air. What I can tell you is which team has more points, and that is the column the standings are using right now.

Dana Whitlock's Grinders are four games clear of the sixth seed. Four games, seven to play. That's the segment's first row.

## The Live Ones, In Games And In Points

Pop quiz: how many teams are separated by one game in this field? Answer: six. The Nightjars and Harriers at 4-3, the Foxes and Mudlarks at 3-4, and the Anvils and Pilots at 2-5. One game covers seeds four through nine on the win column.

So the win column is not going to sort this. Go to points. The Foxes hold the sixth seed at 720.7. The Mudlarks are seventh at 705.2. Difference: 720.7 minus 705.2, 15.5 points. That is the entire wall between in and out at the moment — fifteen and a half points across seven games.

Behind them, the Ashgrove Anvils at 2-5 and 688.6, on a three-game losing streak, and the Pike County Pilots at 2-5 and 664.1, losing two straight. Both a game back of the Foxes at 3-4. Neither is eliminated. Both need help.

One note on the week that produced this row: the Nightjars were projected 126.2 and scored 103.8, a shortfall of 22.4 points, and lost to the Harriers by 23.7. Projected is a suggestion. The box score is a verdict. Reggie will tell you the scoreboard is the only column that matters. The scoreboard is one column. The other nine rows are why the Nightjars now share fourth-and-fifth with a team on a two-game winning streak.

Meanwhile the Cormorants beat the Anvils by 45.2. That is the only blowout on the board this week. Everything else was inside 23.7.

## Show Your Work: The Elimination Arithmetic

Fourteen regular-season weeks, seven played. Fourteen minus seven: seven games left for everyone. Write that on the board, because every number below comes from it.

The Grinders: 7-0 with seven remaining. Their worst possible finish is 7-7. Their current lead over the sixth seed is four games — 7-0 against the Foxes at 3-4. Not clinched. Nothing is clinched with seven games left, and I will not pretend the arithmetic says otherwise.

The Sable Ridge Sentinels: 0-7 with seven remaining. Best possible finish, 7-7. The Cormorants and Quakers already have five wins with seven to play, so their floor is 5-9. The Sentinels can still reach seven. So no, not mathematically eliminated. Seven consecutive losses is not an elimination number; it is a warning label.

The Anvils and Pilots: 2-5, seven left, ceiling of 9-5. Alive.

And the row I keep coming back to: the Sentinels lost by 5.3 with 78.6 points on their bench, the highest bench total on the week. Duke Ellery was benched. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, a gain of 17.9 points. The margin was 5.3. That is, and I am using the technical term, a fucking problem. Moving on.

## What Week 8 Does To The Board

Everyone look at week 8, because the schedule handed us the one game that actually moves the top.

Grinders host Cormorants. 7-0 against 5-2. Projected 124.8 to 122.1 — a projected gap of 2.7 points, which is the numerical equivalent of a shrug. The Grinders have won the only previous meeting between these two, one to nothing. A Cormorants win makes it 7-1 and 6-2 and puts the first seed inside one game. That is the whole week in one line.

Quakers host Nightjars: 5-2 and 761.3 against 4-3 and 744.8, and the visitors are the higher projection at 119.4 to 116.9. Harriers host Foxes, the five seed against the six, projected 117.6 for the visitors against 113.2. Both of those games are seeding, directly.

Mudlarks host Anvils, 3-4 against 2-5, projected 110.5 to 104.3, and the Anvils have won the only prior meeting one to nothing. Pilots host Sentinels, 2-5 against 0-7, and the 0-7 team is the higher projection at 112.9 to 108.7.

Projected is a suggestion. I said it about the Nightjars and I will say it here. Walt will tell you what it all means. I have the rows. That's the segment.
