# Seven And Oh At The Top, Oh And Seven At The Bottom

## Good Evening From The Ironclad Desk

Good evening. The Ironclad Fantasy Conference is seven weeks into its 2025 season, ten teams, full PPR, and exactly one of them has not lost a game.

That is the Gravel Pit Grinders at 7-0, on a seven-game winning streak, with 812.4 points for. At the other end of the board, the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 with 651.9 points for.

We should say plainly what we do not have. There are no previous seasons on file here, no all-time records, no championship history. The archive is empty; the desk is as surprised as you are. So the history of this league is seven weeks old, and we will read it as it stands.

Six teams make the playoffs out of fourteen regular-season weeks. That means the cut line currently sits at the Foundry District Foxes, 3-4. Half the league is one week from being in it.

Let's go to the board.

## The Week In Things That Weren't Supposed To Happen

One official upset on the board. The Halyard Bay Harriers went to the Ninth Street Nightjars and won 127.5 to 103.8, a margin of 23.7. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and beat that by 18.1. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and missed by 22.4. That is how a comfortable win happens to the wrong team.

Halyard Bay is now 4-3 on a two-game streak and sits fifth, 11.3 points for behind Ninth Street's 744.8. The seeding does not reflect the scoreboard yet.

Closest game: the Grinders over the Sentinels by 5.3, and we have already covered the 17.9 that was sitting still.

No comebacks to report beyond that. The other three games were decided by 45.2, 21.8 and 6.4. Reggie will tell you only the margins in the win column matter, and he is up next.
