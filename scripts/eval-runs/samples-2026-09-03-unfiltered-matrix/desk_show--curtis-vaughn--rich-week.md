# The Five-Point Bench: Sable Ridge Falls Again

## Cold Open

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in this league beat the only winless one by 5.3 points, and the loser's bench outscored its winner's bench 78.6 to 41.3.

One question on the desk tonight: did Sable Ridge lose that game, or did Gravel Pit win it? Let's go to the board.

## Opening Statements

Mel, I'm told, has opinions, and she opens with a single number: 17.9. That is the point gain Sable Ridge Sentinels forfeited by benching Duke Ellery in place of Cole Vandermeer, who started and scored 6.2. Ellery scored 24.1 on the bench. The margin was 5.3. Mel's position, stated once and loudly: the game was decided at noon on Sunday, not on the field.

Reggie takes the other chair and refuses to look at the bench at all. His number is 7-0-0. His second number is 812.4 points for, most in the league. Rex Dolan went for 31.8 against a projection of 22.4 for the Grinders, and Reggie's argument is that a team that scores 134.2 does not need anybody else's mistake. The scoreboard is all that matters, and he will tell you so himself.

Two takes. One board. Stay with us.

## The Verdict

Nina grades both takes, and she splits them. Mel gets the round on the game: 17.9 forfeited on a 5.3-point margin is the whole story of that box score. Reggie gets the round on the season: 7-0-0 and 812.4 points for is not an accident, and neither is 0-7-0 and 651.9.

Winner on the night, per Nina: Mel, by the width of the bench.

And the season record from this desk, read straight. In Week 4 we said Cedar Falls finishes top two in points for. They sit second at 788.9. That one landed. In Week 5 we said Sable Ridge Sentinels do not win a game before Thanksgiving. They are 0-7-0, and that one is still open.

We'll have more on that after the break.

Mel's jab: a team can leave 78.6 on the bench or it can complain about a five-point loss, not both.

Mine is a prediction, and it is short. Cedar Falls Cormorants beat Gravel Pit Grinders in Week 8 and this league gets its first look at a one-loss leader.
