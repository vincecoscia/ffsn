# Gravel Pit Grinders Take Week 7 Unbeaten, By Five Points

## Good Evening. Here's Where We Stand.

Good evening. Through seven weeks of the Ironclad Fantasy Conference, one team has not lost and one team has not won, and on Sunday they played each other to within five and three tenths.

Let's go to the board. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points, the most in the league. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 with 651.9, the fewest. Between them sit eight teams separated by five games and 137.3 points — 788.9 for the Cedar Falls Cormorants down to 664.1 for the Pike County Pilots.

Six of ten make the playoffs in a 14-week regular season. That means the Foundry District Foxes and Milltown Mudlarks, both 3-4, are currently on the right side of a line the Ashgrove Anvils and Pike County are not.

We are seven weeks into fourteen. Halfway is a word people use loosely. Stay with us.
