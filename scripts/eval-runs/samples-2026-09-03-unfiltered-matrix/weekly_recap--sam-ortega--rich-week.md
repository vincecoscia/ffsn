# Five Points and a Bench: Gravel Pit Grinders Stay Perfect

## Notebook open, Week 7

I am on the field after the last final. Week 7 is done and the top of this league did not move.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0. The Cedar Falls Cormorants and the Quarry Road Quakers are both 5-2. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 and lost this one by 5.3.

I have three managers on the record and two who are not. I ask everybody the same first question: what do you want to say about it. Then I ask the second one.

Nina has the rest of the numbers. I have the people.

## Five point three, and the bench behind it

The Grinders 134.2, the Sentinels 128.9. Margin 5.3.

Rex Dolan went for 31.8 for the Grinders. Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 for the Sentinels. That part was even.

The part that was not: Duke Ellery was on the Sentinels bench and scored 24.1. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and scored 6.2. That is a gain of 17.9 points on a game decided by 5.3.

I ask Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels why Ellery sat.

I ask again, plainly, because I owe her the harder question and not a soft one: does 17.9 change the answer.

:::quote{id=Q2}

That is the whole exchange. She answered both times, on the record, standing there, at 0-7. I write it down as said.

Down the line, I ask Dana Whitlock of the Gravel Pit Grinders about being seven and zero.

## Walking the rest of the line

**Halyard Bay 127.5, Ninth Street 103.8.** The upset of the week, and it was not close: 23.7. Gil Provost put up 29.9 for the Harriers. Tobias Reneau started for the Nightjars and got 8.1. The Nightjars left 61.5 on the bench; I asked about it and got no reply before I filed.

I find Lena Okafor of the Halyard Bay Harriers first. I ask if she expected it.

:::quote{id=Q4}

I ask her what she did care about. She says the two-game streak. That is a fair answer to the question I asked.

**Cedar Falls 141.6, Ashgrove 96.4.** A 45.2 blowout, the biggest score of the week. Cass Lindqvist went 33.4 for the Cormorants. Bo Tremaine started for the Anvils and returned 4.8. Bea Coleridge of the Ashgrove Anvils declined to comment on a request sent Monday. That is her right, and it is a shorter paragraph than most.

**Quarry Road 118.7, Milltown 112.3.** Six point four. Femi Adeyemi was the best player on the field, 26.3 for the Mudlarks, and his team still lost. Nico Barrantes went 19.7 for the Quakers. The Mudlarks claimed Rudy Halvorsen off waivers Wednesday morning for 34. Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks did not respond to a request for comment sent Monday. I asked Monday. I asked again Tuesday. It is Wednesday.

**Foundry District 121.4, Pike County 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 for the Foxes. Ellis Marchetti, 12.5 for the Pilots, who added Nate Brill and dropped Kip Whorley on Wednesday. No quote from either side. I stood there for a while.

Week 8 puts the Grinders and the Cormorants together at the top. That is the one I will be walking toward.

## Who I chased, who I couldn't find

Cass Lindqvist, 33.4, in a 141.6 team score. Nobody scored more this week. Rex Dolan, 31.8, in a game his team won by 5.3, which makes him the reason.

Gil Provost, 29.9, in the upset. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6, in a loss. Femi Adeyemi, 26.3, also in a loss — two of the four biggest days of the week belong to teams that went home 0-1.

The other end. Bo Tremaine started and scored 4.8. Cole Vandermeer started and scored 6.2 in a five-point loss. Tobias Reneau started and scored 8.1 in a 23.7 defeat.

Duke Ellery scored 24.1 and it counted for nothing, because he was benched. His manager answered for that twice, above, which is twice more than most.

I do not grade the lineups. I just write down the numbers and go find whoever set them.

## What they told me

Three managers on the record this week: Ruth Tanaka, Dana Whitlock, Lena Okafor. Their words are up in their own games, where they belong, and my follow-ups are next to them.

Two were not. Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks did not respond to a request for comment sent Monday. Bea Coleridge of the Ashgrove Anvils declined a request sent Monday.

That is five games and three voices. I will ask all five again next week.

This is the part where I hold the mic and wait.
