## Who Should Be Buying, Who Should Be Selling

OPINION, flagged.

Buyers by the standings: Gravel Pit Grinders at 7-0-0, Cedar Falls at 5-2-0, Quarry Road at 5-2-0. Three clubs with eight combined losses between them and a first-place matchup coming Week 8 — Grinders host Cormorants, 812.4 against 788.9, one previous meeting and the Grinders won it.

Sellers by the standings: Sable Ridge at 0-7-0, Pike County and Ashgrove at 2-5-0. That is three teams a combined 4-17-0 and not one listed name between them.

One of those sellers has a running back who put up 28.6 on Sunday in a five-point loss. Nobody has been offered him, because nobody has offered anybody anything.

The deadline talk in this league is theoretical. There is no board to talk about.

## One Read

My read, not reporting: a conference that files one trade in seven weeks is not disciplined, it is disengaged. Three teams sitting at or below two wins with an empty board in late October have made a decision, whether they'd phrase it that way or not. The 34 the Mudlarks spent Wednesday morning is the closest thing to aggression on file. When the paper trail is one deal old enough to have a birthday, the story is the silence.
