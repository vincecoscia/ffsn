What I can tell you is this. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0. They beat the Sable Ridge Sentinels 134.2 to 128.9 on Sunday, a 5.3-point game. I catch Dana Whitlock of the Grinders after the final and ask what seven and zero feels like.

## What Is Actually On The Line

First seed. That's it, and that's plenty. The Grinders are the top seed at 7-0. The Cormorants sit second at 5-2, two games back, which means Week 8 is the only lever Cedar Falls has that doesn't require help.

Six teams make the playoffs. The Quarry Road Quakers are also 5-2 and one Cormorants loss away from the two seed.

Projections have it close: 124.8 for the Grinders at home, 122.1 for the Cormorants.

I ask Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks for a read on the top of the board. Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks did not respond to a request for comment sent Wednesday. Nina has the rest of the numbers. I have the people, and this week one of them has a voicemail box.
