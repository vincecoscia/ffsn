## The Only Deal In The Log

REPORTED: filed Thursday, Oct. 9, half past two in the afternoon. Week 6.

Quarry Road Quakers gave Hank Osterberg. Received Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick.

Foundry District Foxes gave Castellanos and Trawick. Received Osterberg.

Two-for-one. No picks. No cash. That is the whole trade market for this season to date.

Both teams won in Week 7. The Quakers beat the Milltown Mudlarks 118.7 to 112.3, a 6.4 margin. The Foxes beat the Pike County Pilots 121.4 to 99.6, a 21.8 margin.

My read, not reporting: a two-for-one at the deadline-adjacent end of October is a depth trade, not a blockbuster, and both sides walked out of Week 7 with a win, which is the only argument either of them needs to keep the phone on.
