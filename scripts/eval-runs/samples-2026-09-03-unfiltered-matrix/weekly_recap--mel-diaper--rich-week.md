# Seven And Oh, And A Bench That Cost Sable Ridge The Game

## WEEK SEVEN AND I AM ALREADY YELLING

SEVEN AND OH. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points and they just won by FIVE POINT THREE. FIVE. Six teams make the playoffs in this conference and after seven weeks the line is drawn at 3-4, which means the Foundry District Foxes are holding the last ticket by a fingernail and the Milltown Mudlarks at 3-4 with 705.2 points are outside looking in on a tiebreaker of pure production. Meanwhile the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 on an L7 streak and left 78.6 points on the bench in a five-point loss. SEVENTY-EIGHT POINT SIX. I want a hearing, I want a bailiff, and I want the lineup card entered into evidence.

## GAME OF THE WEEK: FIVE POINTS AND SEVENTY-EIGHT POINTS OF SHAME

Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2. Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Margin: 5.3.

And on the Sentinels bench: 78.6 points. The Grinders benched 41.3, which means Sable Ridge sat 37.3 MORE POINTS than the undefeated team did (78.6 minus 41.3). THAT IS THE WHOLE BALLGAME AND IT IS BULLSHIT.

Here is the receipt with the bow on it. Duke Ellery was benched. Ellery went for 24.1 against a projection of 11.7, and he would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and put up SIX POINT TWO against a 15.8 projection. The gain from making that one swap: 17.9 points. The margin was 5.3. SEVENTEEN POINT NINE VERSUS FIVE POINT THREE. You do not need a calculator, you need a mirror.

Ruth Tanaka went on the record about the bench, and we will get to it, LOUDLY.

What kills me is that Sable Ridge did the hard part. Ivan Petrosky ran for 28.6 off a 14.9 projection — nearly DOUBLE — and the Sentinels beat their own projection of 118.3 by 10.6. They out-projected nothing and still lost, because Rex Dolan put 31.8 on the board for the Grinders against a 22.4 projection and Dana Whitlock's team has now done this SEVEN TIMES.

I am formally demanding the commissioner impound that Sentinels lineup card and mail it back framed. Two outrageous asks? Fine: Sable Ridge should be barred from touching the bench toggle until December.

And Milltown burned 34 on Rudy Halvorsen Wednesday morning and STILL came up 6.4 short — I want that waiver claim reviewed by a panel of strangers.

HALYARD BAY 127.5, NINTH STREET 103.8. THE MUGGING. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and scored 103.8 — that is 22.4 points of NOTHING (126.2 minus 103.8) — while stacking 61.5 on the bench. Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6. Dane Kowalczyk 27.2 off a 16.8 projection. THE LAST TICKET IS BEING HELD BY A 3-4 TEAM AND NOBODY IS PANICKING BUT ME.

Dane Kowalczyk 27.2, Femi Adeyemi 26.3, and Nico Barrantes 19.7 off a 10.4 projection, which for a tight end is highway robbery in broad daylight.

DUDS. Bo Tremaine, FOUR POINT EIGHT against 16.2. Cole Vandermeer, 6.2 against 15.8, started ahead of a man who went 24.1. Tobias Reneau, 8.1 against 19.3. THREE STARTERS, ELEVEN POINT FOUR TOTAL BETWEEN THE WORST TWO, and every one of those calls was made by a general manager with all week to think about it. WHY THEY PLAYED THAT BADLY IS NOT SOMETHING THE BOX SCORE WILL TELL ME, AND I WANT WHOEVER HAS THAT ANSWER IN FRONT OF A MICROPHONE TONIGHT.
