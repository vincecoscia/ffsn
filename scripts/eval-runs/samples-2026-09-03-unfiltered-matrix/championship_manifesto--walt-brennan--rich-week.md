# Undefeated Is Not a Trophy: The Gravel Pit Grinders and the Five Points That Almost Weren't

## The Setting, Such As It Is

Here is my argument, and I'll put it up front so nobody has to hunt for it: the Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 and they are not seven wins better than anybody, and week 8 against the Cedar Falls Cormorants is the first honest appraisal this league has scheduled all year. A season is a mortgage. You do not own the house because you made the payments on time; you own the interest you have not paid yet. The Grinders have made every payment — 812.4 points, more than anyone — and they made the last one Sunday by 5.3, which is the sound of a check clearing at 4:59 on a Friday.

I agree with Dana Whitlock, which is a hell of a way to open a column, and I still think the number is doing more work than the roster underneath it. Seven and oh is a receipt. Nobody hangs a receipt on a wall.

## The Grinders Have Paid Every Bill On Time

What the Grinders have is Rex Dolan, who went for 31.8 against a projection of 22.4, and a general manager who has not blinked. That is not a small thing. I have watched teams in this league win six and look like they were apologizing for it; Dana Whitlock's team wins and then goes back to work, and the waiver discipline I credited in week 4 has not slipped since.

But 134.2 against 128.9 is not a victory, it is a near miss with a bow on it, and the 41.3 the Grinders left sitting down says the roster is deeper than the lineup card has needed it to be. That is a compliment right now. In November it is a question.

The week 8 opponent is the one team with real standing to ask it. Cedar Falls sits at 5-2 with 788.9 points, which is 812.4 minus 788.9 — 23.5 points — behind the Grinders on the year, and their week was the cleanest thing on the board: 141.6 to 96.4, Cass Lindqvist for 33.4 against a 17.6 projection, an afternoon that never required a decision. The Cormorants are the only team here whose scoring can look the Grinders in the eye without squinting. I said in week 4 that Cedar Falls would finish top two in points for and I am not walking any of that back.

The Grinders are projected 124.8 to Cedar Falls' 122.1 on Sunday, and the head-to-head record between them is one win to nothing in the Grinders' favor. That is a margin you could sneeze away. I like the Grinders anyway; I like managers who decide things, and Dana Whitlock has decided seven times running.

A plan that has not been tested is not a plan. It is a hope with good posture.

## Sable Ridge, Once, In Full

Now the other half of that 5.3, because it is the more interesting half and because I promised myself I'd say this once and be done.

The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 and they scored 128.9 on Sunday, which is more than three winners on the same slate managed. Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 off a 14.9 projection. And Duke Ellery was benched — he would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, and starting him was worth 17.9 points, in a game the Sentinels lost by 5.3. A bench is a confession, and this one confessed at volume: 78.6 points sat down.

I have been hard on the Sentinels this season and Ruth Tanaka has been hard right back, and I will say plainly that the second of those two answers is the most honest thing any manager has told this desk all year. The first one is still the wrong call, dressed in a reason. Fear of a zero is how you talk yourself into a shitty lineup and call it caution; you do not protect a 0-7 team by hedging, you protect it by swinging.

I said in week 5 that Sable Ridge does not win a game before Thanksgiving. That prediction is still open, and Sunday is the closest anyone has come to closing it against me. The Sentinels draw the Pike County Pilots in week 8 and are projected 112.9 to 108.7 — the first time all season the numbers have been on their side.

That is my last word on Sable Ridge this season. Ruth Tanaka can hold me to it.

## What Sunday Actually Requires

The Grinders need Rex Dolan to keep outrunning his projection, because 31.8 against a 22.4 forecast is not a weekly service and a projection is a weather forecast read by someone who never goes outside. They also need their lineup card to catch up to their roster; you cannot leave 41.3 behind twice against this opponent.

Cedar Falls needs the version of the afternoon it just had — Cass Lindqvist above 30 again, nothing asked of the bench, 52.7 points allowed to stay comfortable. Marcus Bly's team has the scoring. It has never had to spend it on anything that mattered.

I'll take the Grinders by less than they'd like. Curtis and Nina have the rest of the column; I only wanted these two numbers.

## What It Buys Them

Nothing yet. This league's record only goes back as far as it goes back, and 7-0 in October has never hung in a rafter anywhere. What the Grinders are building is credit. Credit is not money. Somebody finds that out every November, and I've done this long enough not to bet on who.
