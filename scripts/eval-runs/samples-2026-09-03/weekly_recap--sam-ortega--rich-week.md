# Five Points, One Bench, and a Manager Who Knows Exactly What That Means

## Down on the field, Week 7

The field empties out and one number follows me around all night: 5.3.

That's the margin between the Gravel Pit Grinders, now 7-0, and the Sable Ridge Sentinels, now 0-7. First seed against tenth. Decided by less than a touchdown.

Elsewhere, Halyard Bay took the upset, Cedar Falls took a blowout, and Quarry Road survived by 6.4. Six teams make the playoffs here, and the gap between the fourth and sixth seeds is one game.

Nina has the rest of the numbers. I have the people. Two of them declined to be people this week, and I'll get to that.

## Grinders 134.2, Sentinels 128.9

I catch Dana Whitlock of the Gravel Pit Grinders after the final. Seven and zero. I ask what that feels like.

:::quote{id=Q3}

Rex Dolan went for 31.8 for the Grinders, against a projection of 22.4. Whitlock's bench held 41.3. That is not the bench anyone wants to talk about.

The other one is 78.6, and it belongs to the Sentinels.

Duke Ellery was on the Sable Ridge bench for 24.1 points. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and scored 6.2. The gain was 17.9. The margin was 5.3.

I ask Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels about Ellery.

:::quote{id=Q1}

I ask again, plainly, whether she'd seen the final margin before she answered.

:::quote{id=Q2}

That is the whole exchange and I am printing all of it. Tanaka and I have had sharper weeks. This one she answered twice.

Ivan Petrosky ran for 28.6 for Sable Ridge against a 14.9 projection, which is the sentence a 0-7 team gets to keep. Seven losses, seven weeks.

## The other four, in the order the people were standing

**Halyard Bay 127.5, Ninth Street 103.8.** The upset of the week, and the projections agreed it shouldn't have happened: Ninth Street was projected 126.2, Halyard Bay 109.4. Gil Provost went for 29.9 for the Harriers. Tobias Reneau started for Ninth Street and got 8.1 against a 19.3 projection.

I ask Lena Okafor of the Halyard Bay Harriers about being the underdog.

:::quote{id=Q4}

Halyard Bay is 4-3 and on a two-game run. Ninth Street sits 4-3 with a loss on the board. Trevor Ashby was not on the record this week; I cover the game without him.

**Cedar Falls 141.6, Ashgrove 96.4.** The biggest score of the week and the biggest margin, 45.2. Cass Lindqvist put up 33.4 for the Cormorants. Bo Tremaine started for Ashgrove and finished with 4.8 against a 16.2 projection.

Bea Coleridge of the Ashgrove Anvils declined to comment. That is a complete answer and I have written down worse ones.

**Quarry Road 118.7, Milltown 112.3.** Six-point-four apart. Femi Adeyemi was the best player on the field, 26.3 for the Mudlarks, and it did not matter. Nico Barrantes went 19.7 for the Quakers from tight end, nearly double a 10.4 projection.

Milltown claimed Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning for 34 dollars, dropping Pete Sandoval. I wanted to ask about that.

Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks did not respond to a request for comment sent Monday. Half a conversation again.

**Foundry District 121.4, Pike County 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk carried the Foxes with 27.2. Ellis Marchetti led Pike County with 12.5, under his 15.4 projection. Pike County left 55.3 on the bench against the Foxes' 36.4, a difference of 18.9. No one from either side went on the record.

## Who showed up and who didn't

Top of the week: Cass Lindqvist, 33.4 for Cedar Falls. Rex Dolan, 31.8 for the Grinders. Gil Provost, 29.9 for Halyard Bay. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6 for a team that is 0-7.

The best player on a losing side was Femi Adeyemi, 26.3 for Milltown in a game decided by 6.4. His manager did not respond to a request for comment sent Monday, so nobody gets to enjoy that out loud.

The other end. Bo Tremaine started for Ashgrove and finished with 4.8 against a 16.2 projection, in a 45.2-point loss. Tobias Reneau started for Ninth Street and got 8.1 against 19.3, in the upset. Cole Vandermeer started for Sable Ridge and got 6.2 against 15.8, with Duke Ellery and his 24.1 sitting behind him.

I asked about the ones I could ask about. Nobody from Ashgrove, Ninth Street or Pike County went on the record about a starter this week. Nina has the rest of the numbers. I have the people who talked.

## What they told me

Whitlock gave me one line about 7-0 and walked. Okafor gave me one line about winning a game she was projected to lose. Tanaka gave me two, both about the same bench, and did not ask me to take either back.

Radcliffe did not respond to a request for comment sent Monday. Coleridge declined.

Week 8 puts the two best teams in the league in the same game, the Grinders and the Cormorants, and I already know which bench I'm asking about.

This is the part where I hold the mic and wait.
