# Seven And Oh, By Five Points

## Good Evening

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in this league beat the only winless team in this league by five and three tenths, and we will call that the state of the conference.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7. They played the closest game of the week against each other, which is the kind of thing the schedule does when nobody is watching.

Five games, one upset, one blowout. Cedar Falls Cormorants put up the week's high score at 141.6. Six playoff spots, seven weeks gone, and the bottom half is still within a game of the line.

Let's go to the board.

## Game Of The Week: 134.2 To 128.9

The Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, the Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Margin of 5.3. Tightest game on the board, and everything else was decided by more than four times that.

Rex Dolan of the Grinders led all scorers on the day with 31.8 against a projection of 22.4. Ivan Petrosky answered for Sable Ridge with 28.6 on a 14.9 projection. That is a game.

Now the part the desk regrets to report. Sable Ridge benched Duke Ellery, who scored 24.1. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer in the lineup, and the swap was worth 17.9 points. The final margin was 5.3.

Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels went on the record about it, and we will get to that in the comments segment.

Sable Ridge also put up 78.6 points on the bench to the Grinders' 41.3. Nina has the bench math and I am told it is not a short segment.

Dana Whitlock of the Gravel Pit Grinders is now 7-0 with a seven-game winning streak and 812.4 points for, the most in the league. That is a record, and it earned the extra beat.

Stay with us.

## Around The Board

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** The week's high score against the week's low score, margin 45.2. Cass Lindqvist went for 33.4 on a 17.6 projection. Bo Tremaine of the Anvils returned 4.8 on a 16.2 projection. Ashgrove has lost three straight and sits 2-5. We reached out to Ashgrove Anvils and did not hear back; Bea Coleridge declined to comment. Noted.

Cedar Falls also claimed Trey Ballinger off waivers for 12 dollars this week. Dex is working the phones on whether that one matters.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Second-closest game of the day at 6.4. Femi Adeyemi was the best player on the field with 26.3, and he plays for the losing side. Nico Barrantes gave the Quakers 19.7 from tight end against a 10.4 projection, which is where that game turned. Milltown was the only side projected to win and lose in this one — 119.8 projected, 112.3 actual. Owen Radcliffe of the Milltown Mudlarks did not respond to our comment request. Noted.

The Mudlarks also spent 34 dollars on Rudy Halvorsen at waivers Wednesday morning. We'll leave that there.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The upset of the week. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and finished 103.8. Gil Provost carried Halyard Bay with 29.9. Tobias Reneau managed 8.1 for the Nightjars on a 19.3 projection. Ninth Street's bench outscored Halyard Bay's, 61.5 to 33.8. Halyard Bay has now won two in a row and moved to 4-3. Lena Okafor of the Harriers had something to say, and it is in the next segment.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Margin 21.8. Dane Kowalczyk went 27.2 for the Foxes against a 16.8 projection. Ellis Marchetti led Pike County with 12.5. The Pilots have lost two straight and added Nate Brill at tight end Wednesday. That is the whole of it.
