# Class, Circle the Bench Column: Week 7 Power Rankings

## Class, Take Your Seats

Class. Seven games. That is the sample for everything below, so hold it loosely. I am holding it loosely. I am also still right.

Circle the top line: Gravel Pit Grinders, 7-0, 812.4 points scored, first in the league. Then circle the bottom line, because it is the more interesting row. Sable Ridge Sentinels lost by 5.3 while their bench scored 78.6. Reggie downstairs will tell you the scoreboard is the only column. The scoreboard is one column. The bench is the one that explains it.

Elsewhere: Cedar Falls Cormorants posted the week's biggest number at 141.6. Halyard Bay Harriers went into Ninth Street projected 109.4, scored 127.5, and won by 23.7. The projection was a suggestion. The box score is a verdict.

## The Board, Top to Bottom

**1. Gravel Pit Grinders (7-0).** 134.2 scored, 121.6 projected. Rex Dolan, their quarterback, went 31.8 on a 22.4 projection. Was this a dominant week? It was not. They won by 5.3. Unbeaten and narrow are allowed to be true in the same row.

**2. Cedar Falls Cormorants (5-2).** 141.6, top score of the week, and 45.2 clear of Ashgrove. Cass Lindqvist: 33.4 on a 17.6 projection. That is a real column. I checked it twice. I noted in Week 4 that Cedar Falls would finish top two in points for; 788.9, second in the league, and that one landed.

**3. Quarry Road Quakers (5-2).** 118.7 to 112.3, margin 6.4. Nico Barrantes, their tight end, went 19.7 on a 10.4 projection — the entire difference and then some. Quarry Road also owns the only trade on the books, back in Week 6: they sent out Hank Osterberg and took back Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick. Verdict on that trade is not in front of me, so I am not pretending otherwise.

**4. Ninth Street Nightjars (4-3).** Projected 126.2, scored 103.8. That is the largest gap between suggestion and verdict on the board. Tobias Reneau, 8.1 on a 19.3 projection. Their bench scored 61.5, which is a number, though nothing in front of me flags a specific lineup swap there. Pop quiz: which is the bigger problem, the projection or the receiver? The receiver has a name.

**5. Halyard Bay Harriers (4-3).** The week's only upset, and the only team on this board whose bench I have nothing rude to say about — 33.8, lowest bench total of the week. Gil Provost, 29.9 on a 20.7 projection. Efficient. Underline it.

**6. Foundry District Foxes (3-4).** 121.4 to 99.6. Dane Kowalczyk went 27.2 on a 16.8 projection. Sixth seed, four losses, 720.7 points. The record says mediocre; the scoring column says middle of a tight pack. Both rows are true.

**7. Milltown Mudlarks (3-4).** Projected 119.8, scored 112.3, lost by 6.4. Femi Adeyemi did his part at 26.3 on an 18.1 projection. Milltown also spent 34 on Rudy Halvorsen at Wednesday morning waivers, dropping Pete Sandoval. Milltown didn't comment, so this is the box score's version, which is the version I'd have used anyway. And credit where the column gives it: they were the better projected team and lost by less than seven. That's a coin flip, not a collapse.

**8. Ashgrove Anvils (2-5).** 96.4 on a 108.2 projection, three straight losses. Bo Tremaine, 4.8 on a 16.2 projection. Ashgrove declined to comment; that is a fact, not a clue.

**9. Pike County Pilots (2-5).** 99.6 scored, 107.9 projected, bench 55.3. Two straight losses and last-but-one in points at 664.1.

**10. Sable Ridge Sentinels (0-7).** Here is the cleanest lesson of the week. Duke Ellery was benched. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who scored 6.2 on a 15.8 projection. The gain: 17.9. The loss: 5.3. Show your work — 17.9 is larger than 5.3. That is the segment, and it happened in the tenth row of the standings.

## Movement, With The Number Attached

Up: Halyard Bay. Projected 109.4, scored 127.5, beat a team projected 126.2. Two straight wins, fifth seed, and the tidiest bench total on the slate at 33.8. Mel will tell you a pick number is a personality. Lena Okafor's team just told you it is a lineup.

Down: Ninth Street. Fourth seed, first loss in the streak column, and a 22.4-point shortfall against their own projection — 126.2 minus 103.8. That is not schedule. Schedules do not miss projections by twenty-two.

Sideways and undefeated: Gravel Pit, who won by 5.3 and still lead the league by 23.5 points scored over Cedar Falls — 812.4 against 788.9. Seven games. Loosely held. Still first.
