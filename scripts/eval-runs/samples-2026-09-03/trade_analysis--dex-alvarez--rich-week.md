# Two-For-One, No Picks: The Only Trade On The Books

## Here's What I've Got

REPORTED: Quarry Road Quakers to Foundry District Foxes. Filed Thursday, Oct. 9, half past two in the afternoon.

Two-for-one. No picks. No cash.

Quarry Road gave Hank Osterberg. Quarry Road received Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick.

That is the entire trade. That is also the entire trade market. One deal on the books all season.

REPORTED: Quarry Road sits third at five and two, 761.3 points for.

They went from one back on the depth sheet to none, and from one wideout and one tight end to two of each. Castellanos is on the roster. Trawick came in the same envelope.

REPORTED: Week 7, Quarry Road 118.7, Milltown 112.3. Margin 6.4. Nico Barrantes went for 19.7 against a projection of 10.4.

Priya Nandi did not appear on the record this week. No quote exists on this deal from her side, and I am not going to build one.

OPINION: giving up your only listed back to fill two other holes is a choice you make when you think the season is long. Seven weeks in, third place, it has not cost them a game I can point to.

## Foundry District's Side Of The Paper

REPORTED: Foundry District gave Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick. They received Hank Osterberg. Filed Thursday, Oct. 9.

REPORTED: the Foxes are sixth. Three and four. 720.7 points for.

They came out of the deal with two running backs — Osterberg and Dane Kowalczyk. That is the roster I can account for. No wideout, no tight end I can name on their side.

REPORTED: Week 7, Foundry District 121.4, Pike County 99.6. Margin 21.8. Kowalczyk 27.2 against a projection of 16.8.

Sam Kestrel did not go on the record this week. Nobody from Foundry District did. I asked; nothing came back, and I am not filling that in for them.

OPINION: two-for-one going out is a trade you make when you believe one name beats two. Three and four says the jury is still seated.

## The Grade, Flat

Quarry Road: B-plus. Foundry District: B-minus. Those are the grades as filed.

REPORTED: Quarry Road is 5-2, third. Foundry District is 3-4, sixth. Both have won their last game.
