# FOURTEEN PICKS OF AIR: Mel Diaper Grades The Ironclad Draft

## THIRTY PICKS. THIRTY RECEIPTS. LET'S GO.

Thirty picks are on the board in the Ironclad Fantasy Conference and I have EVERY ONE OF THEM in my hand. Ten teams. PPR. Snake. Three rounds of evidence and I am not letting a single line of it go unpunished.

Here is how this desk grades: projected starter points, the pick number against ADP, and what is sitting behind the starters. THAT'S IT. If you took a man EARLIER than the board said, that is a REACH and I will say the word REACH out loud. If you sat there and let him fall to you, that is VALUE and you get credit, whether you meant it or not.

And the biggest reach on this board is FOURTEEN POINT ONE picks. FOURTEEN POINT ONE?! We'll get there. I want you standing when we get there.

One housekeeping item: I asked Ruth Tanaka. Ruth Tanaka has not gotten back to me. I have ALL DAY. Ruth Tanaka also told Sam in Week 14 that I called last season's first-round pick nineteen picks of air. I DID CALL IT THAT. And I brought a bigger number this time.
