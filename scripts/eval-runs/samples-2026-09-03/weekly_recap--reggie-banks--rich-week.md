## Reggie's Week 7 Cold Open

SEVEN AND OH! Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points for, the best number in the building, and they got there Sunday by five-point-three over a team that has not won a game all year. Nobody at this desk cares how it looked. **Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six-point-four. Milltown was the one team projected to win their game and lose it — 119.8 on paper, 112.3 on the field. Tobias Reneau started for the Nightjars at 8.1 against a 19.3 projection, and Ninth Street left 61.5 on the bench. That's a lineup call that did not hit, and I say that with love.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Twenty-one-point-eight. The Pilots are 2-5 and have dropped two straight.

Five games, five results. Four teams beat their projection. Nobody cashed a projection at the bank.
