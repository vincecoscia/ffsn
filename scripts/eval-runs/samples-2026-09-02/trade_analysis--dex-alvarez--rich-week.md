# Quarry Road Sends Osterberg To Foundry District For Two — The Wire On TR1

## REPORTED: the deal, timestamped

Quarry Road Quakers traded Hank Osterberg to the Foundry District Foxes for Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick, filed Thursday, Oct. 9, 2:30 p.m. ET.

One-for-two, no picks, no cash listed. It is the only trade on the league log.

Neither Priya Nandi of the Quarry Road Quakers nor Sam Kestrel of the Foundry District Foxes is in this week's comment ledger, so the reasoning below is need-and-record, not stated intent.

## Quarry Road: two bodies for one

Quarry Road entered Week 7 at 5-2 and sits third with 761.3 points for. They won Sunday, 118.7 to 112.3 over the Milltown Mudlarks, a 6.4-point margin.

Nandi gave up the single asset and took back two. On the roster picture that came through, Quarry Road was carrying nothing at running back and one each at receiver and tight end before the deal, so the two incoming pieces land where the shelf was empty.

The complicating fact: no game log for Osterberg, Castellanos or Trawick came through for this piece. That is a thin paper trail, and it stays thin until somebody publishes the weekly lines for those three.

What did produce for the Quakers in Week 7 was Nico Barrantes, who went for 19.7 against a 10.4 projection as a starter. That is the tight end room they already had.

My read, not reporting: a 5-2 team that trades one for two in October is buying lineup slots, not upside, and the timing on a Thursday afternoon before a Week 6 kickoff points the same way.

Grade as filed: B+.

## Foundry District: one body, the right one

Foundry District went the other way — two out, one in — and did it from 3-4 and sixth place with 720.7 points for.

The Foxes beat the Pike County Pilots 121.4 to 99.6 on Sunday, a 21.8-point margin. Dane Kowalczyk carried the day as a starter with 27.2 points against a 16.8 projection.

On need, the depth read that came through has Foundry District at two running backs and zero at both receiver and tight end before the deal. That is the part of this I keep re-reading: the Foxes shipped out a receiver and a tight end and took back a running back.

Kestrel did not go on the record, and I am not going to build a motive for a manager who did not state one. What I can report is the shape: a sub-.500 team consolidating two starters into one, then winning by 21.8 the next weekend.

Foundry District draws the Halyard Bay Harriers in Week 8, projected 117.6 to 113.2.

Grade as filed: B-.

## OPINION, flagged: the verdict

Quarry Road wins the deal on record and on need. Third place at 5-2, two holes filled, one asset spent.

Foundry District gets the defensible side of it only if Osterberg is the best player in the trade, and nobody published a line to prove that either way.

Grades stand where they were filed: Quarry Road B+, Foundry District B-. Both teams won in Week 7, which is the only scoreboard either manager has to answer to right now.
