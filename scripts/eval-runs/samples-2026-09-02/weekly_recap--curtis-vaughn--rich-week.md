# Gravel Pit Grinders Reach 7-0 by 5.3 Points

## Cold Open

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in the Ironclad Fantasy Conference nearly lost to the only winless one.

Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Margin of 5.3. That moves Dana Whitlock's team to 7-0 and leaves Ruth Tanaka's team at 0-7.

Four of the five games this week were decided by more than that. Cedar Falls Cormorants put up the top score in the league at 141.6 and are 5-2. Quarry Road Quakers held on by 6.4. Halyard Bay Harriers delivered the week's only upset, 127.5 to 103.8. Foundry District Foxes won by 21.8.

The top six seeds stand at Gravel Pit, Cedar Falls, Quarry Road, Ninth Street, Halyard Bay and Foundry District. Full rundown follows, tightest game first.

## Game of the Week: 5.3 Points at the Top and the Bottom

The tightest game on the board was the widest gap in the standings.

Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Both teams cleared their projections — Gravel Pit was set at 121.6, Sable Ridge at 118.3.

Rex Dolan of the Gravel Pit Grinders led all scorers in the game with 31.8 points against a 22.4 projection. Ivan Petrosky answered for Sable Ridge with 28.6 on a 14.9 projection, nearly doubling the number.

The decisive line is on the Sentinels bench. Duke Ellery was benched and scored 24.1 points. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and scored 6.2 against a 15.8 projection. The gain from that swap was 17.9 points. The margin was 5.3.

Sable Ridge's bench outscored Gravel Pit's bench 78.6 to 41.3, a difference of 37.3 points. Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels went on the record about the Ellery decision, and that sound is in the comments section below.

Gravel Pit is 7-0 with 812.4 points for, the most in the league. Sable Ridge is 0-7 with 651.9, the fewest. In Week 5 this desk said Sable Ridge does not win a game before Thanksgiving. That one is still open.

Numbers desk has more on the bench math.

## The Rest of the Rundown

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Margin 6.4. Milltown was the projected side at 119.8 to 114.5 and came in under it. Nico Barrantes went for 19.7 against a 10.4 projection for Quarry Road. Femi Adeyemi was the game's high man at 26.3 for Milltown and it did not travel. Milltown also spent 34 dollars of free-agent budget Wednesday morning on Rudy Halvorsen, dropping Pete Sandoval. Quarry Road is 5-2 and third. Milltown is 3-4. We reached out to the Milltown Mudlarks and did not hear back.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Margin 21.8. Dane Kowalczyk carried Foundry District with 27.2 on a 16.8 projection. Ellis Marchetti led Pike County with 12.5, under his 15.4 projection. Pike County left 55.3 on the bench against Foundry District's 36.4. The Pilots added Nate Brill and dropped Kip Whorley on Wednesday. Foundry District is 3-4 and back in the sixth seed; Pike County is 2-5 and has lost two straight.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The week's upset, by 23.7. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and finished 22.4 under it. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and finished 18.1 over. Gil Provost put up 29.9 for the Harriers. Tobias Reneau managed 8.1 for Ninth Street against a 19.3 projection. Ninth Street's bench scored 61.5, 27.7 more than Halyard Bay's 33.8. Lena Okafor of the Halyard Bay Harriers spoke afterward; that is in the comments section. Halyard Bay is 4-3 and has won two in a row.

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** The largest margin of the week at 45.2, and the highest score of the week at 141.6. Cass Lindqvist did the heavy work with 33.4 against a 17.6 projection. Bo Tremaine started for Ashgrove and scored 4.8 on a 16.2 projection. Cedar Falls claimed Trey Ballinger for 12 dollars Wednesday. The Cormorants are 5-2 with 788.9 points for, second in the league. Ashgrove is 2-5 and has lost three straight. Bea Coleridge of the Ashgrove Anvils declined to comment.

Week 8 puts Gravel Pit and Cedar Falls together, projected 124.8 to 122.1. Gravel Pit holds the only prior meeting, 1-0.
