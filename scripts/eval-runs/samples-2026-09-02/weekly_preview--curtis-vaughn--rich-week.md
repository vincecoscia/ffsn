# Week 8 Preview: Grinders Face Their First Real Test

## What to Watch

Good evening. Gravel Pit Grinders are seven and zero, 812.4 points for, and Sunday they get the first team all season that can match them possession for possession. Cedar Falls Cormorants arrive at five and two off a 141.6 to 96.4 win over Ashgrove Anvils, and the two clubs have met once before this year, with Gravel Pit ahead one game to none in that series. Quarry Road Quakers, at five and two, host Ninth Street Nightjars, who are coming off a loss to Halyard Bay Harriers and need to answer for 61.5 points that never left their bench. Down at the bottom, Sable Ridge Sentinels are zero and seven and get Pike County, who are two and five themselves. Somebody in that game has to win. Nobody has proven they know how yet. Every score below is a projection, not a result, because none of these five games has been played.

## The Marquee Matchup

Gravel Pit Grinders host Cedar Falls Cormorants with the top spot in the standings effectively already Dana Whitlock's, but this is the closest projection on the board: 124.8 to 122.1, a projection and nothing more. The Grinders needed a nail-biter to get past Sable Ridge last week, winning 134.2 to 128.9. The Cormorants had no such trouble, running past Ashgrove 141.6 to 96.4 with 52.7 points sitting unused on their bench. History favors the home team here — Gravel Pit is 1-0 against Cedar Falls this season. Whitlock was asked about the significance of starting seven and zero.

## Around the League

Quarry Road Quakers, fresh off a 118.7 to 112.3 win over Milltown Mudlarks, host Ninth Street Nightjars, who fell to Halyard Bay 103.8 to 127.5 and left 61.5 points on their bench in the process. Projection has Ninth Street escaping by a nose, 119.4 to 116.9. Halyard Bay Harriers, winners of that upset over Ninth Street, now host Foundry District Foxes, who beat Pike County 121.4 to 99.6 last week. The projection likes the Foxes on the road, 117.6 to 113.2. Milltown Mudlarks host Ashgrove Anvils in a rematch of a game Ashgrove already won this season, though both clubs enter off losses — Milltown fell to Quarry Road, Ashgrove to Cedar Falls. Projection favors Milltown at home, 110.5 to 104.3. And Pike County Pilots host Sable Ridge Sentinels in a game between the league's last two teams in the standings. The projection has Sable Ridge on top, 112.9 to 108.7, which would be their first win of the season if it holds — and it is only a projection.

## Under the Radar

The name from last week that should stick with anyone previewing Sable Ridge is Duke Ellery, kept on the bench while Cole Vandermeer started and managed just 6.2 points. Ellery would have replaced Vandermeer for a gain of 17.9 points, per the benching math, in a game the Sentinels lost by 5.3. That decision is Sable Ridge's story heading into the Pike County game as much as anything on the projection sheet. Elsewhere, keep an eye on whether Ninth Street's bench problem — 61.5 points unused last week — repeats itself against Quarry Road. Same for Milltown, who left 47.2 on the bench in their loss.
