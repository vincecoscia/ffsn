# Five Points and a Bench Full of Receipts: Week 7 in the Ironclad

## Mel's Cold Open: Nobody Is Undefeated By Accident

Seven and zero. That is Gravel Pit Grinders, 812.4 points for, the top seed in this conference and the only team in it that has not lost. And they nearly lost this one.

Week 7 gave us a 5.3-point nail-biter at the top, a 45.2-point beating in the two-seed's living room, and an upset in which the favorite by 22.4 projected points lost by 23.7. Three games decided inside 6.5 points or under it. Two decided by more than 21.

And the story of the week is not a stat line. It is a bench. Sable Ridge left 78.6 points on the bench in a game they lost by 5.3. That is the receipt. We are going to read it out loud.

## Game of the Week: Grinders 134.2, Sentinels 128.9

Rex Dolan, quarterback, Gravel Pit Grinders: 31.8 points on a 22.4 projection. That is 9.4 points over the number, and it is the entire reason Dana Whitlock is 7-0 instead of 6-1.

Sable Ridge did not lose this game on talent. Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 against a 14.9 projection — 13.7 over. The Sentinels beat their own projection, 128.9 against 118.3. They lost anyway, and here is why: Duke Ellery was benched, he scored 24.1 points, and the sheet says he would have replaced Cole Vandermeer for a gain of 17.9. Vandermeer started and put up 6.2 on a 15.8 projection. The margin was 5.3.

Ruth Tanaka told Sam in Week 6 that the desk should try setting a lineup once. Ruth, I would love to. I would start the 24.1 over the 6.2. That is the whole job.

The Sentinels also dropped Sonny Vega on Friday evening, two days before kickoff, which is process, not panic, and I will grant that much.

Dana Whitlock of the Gravel Pit Grinders is the one manager in this league whose calls I let stand. Waiver discipline in Week 4, 812.4 points for now, and a quarterback beating his projection by 9.4. That is not luck, that is a build.

Sable Ridge is 0-7 on an L7 streak. In Week 5 I said they do not win a game before Thanksgiving. That one is still open, and after a 5.3-point loss to the best team in the league, I am not moving off it.

## The Other Four: Blowouts, Upsets and One Quiet Six-Pointer

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Highest score of the week, 45.2-point margin, and Cass Lindqvist did the heavy lifting with 33.4 on a 17.6 projection — 15.8 over the number and the biggest single overage on the board. Cedar Falls also spent 12 on Trey Ballinger on Wednesday morning. Twelve dollars. That is a manager who knows exactly what a bench spot is worth. Back in Week 4 I called Cedar Falls a top-two points-for team and at 788.9, second in the league, that one landed.

Ashgrove is on an L3 streak at 2-5 and got 4.8 points out of Bo Tremaine against a 16.2 projection — 11.4 under. Bea Coleridge declined to comment. That is her right, and I have no more than that.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** The best-played loss of the week. Milltown was the projected favorite, 119.8 to 114.5, and Femi Adeyemi delivered 26.3 on an 18.1 projection. They still lost by 6.4, because Nico Barrantes went 19.7 as a tight end on a 10.4 projection, and 9.3 points of surplus from the thinnest position on the roster is how you win a game you were projected to lose. Priya Nandi is 5-2 and third.

Milltown spent 34 in FAAB on Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning and dropped Pete Sandoval to do it. Thirty-four dollars for a running back on a team that just lost by 6.4 with 47.2 points on its bench. I asked Owen Radcliffe. Owen Radcliffe has not gotten back to me.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The upset. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and scored 103.8 — that is 22.4 under. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and scored 127.5, 18.1 over. Gil Provost put up 29.9 on a 20.7 projection. Tobias Reneau started for Ninth Street and gave back 8.1 on a 19.3 projection, 11.2 under, and Trevor Ashby watched 61.5 points sit on his bench while his lineup underperformed by 22.4. The box score does not tell me which of those bench guys he should have started, and I am annoyed about that, because I would like to name him. Halyard Bay is 4-3 on a W2. Ninth Street is 4-3 on an L1.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 on a 16.8 projection, 10.4 over, and the Foxes never trailed the math. Pike County scored 99.6 against a 107.9 projection with 55.3 on the bench — the second-biggest bench total of the week behind Sable Ridge's 78.6. Ellis Marchetti's 12.5 was the best a Pilots starter managed on this board, and 12.5 does not win games. Hollis Vance swapped Kip Whorley for Nate Brill on Wednesday and is 2-5 on an L2.

## Studs, Duds and the Numbers That Prove It

**Studs.** Cass Lindqvist, Cedar Falls Cormorants: 33.4, projected 17.6, plus-15.8. Best overage of the week and it came in a 45.2-point win, which is the definition of a wasted good day. Rex Dolan, Gravel Pit Grinders: 31.8 on 22.4. Gil Provost, Halyard Bay Harriers: 29.9 on 20.7 in the upset. Ivan Petrosky, Sable Ridge Sentinels: 28.6 on 14.9, nearly doubled his number, and lost. Dane Kowalczyk, Foundry District Foxes: 27.2 on 16.8. Femi Adeyemi, Milltown Mudlarks: 26.3 on 18.1, also lost.

**Duds.** Bo Tremaine, Ashgrove Anvils: 4.8 against a 16.2 projection. That is 11.4 points of nothing in a game his team lost by 45.2, so it did not decide anything, but it is still the worst line on the board. Cole Vandermeer, Sable Ridge Sentinels: 6.2 on 15.8 — 9.6 under, in a game lost by 5.3, with a 24.1-point teammate on the bench. That is the most expensive dud of the season. Tobias Reneau, Ninth Street Nightjars: 8.1 on 19.3.

Fine. I had Nico Barrantes wrong all season and 19.7 out of a 10.4 projection is a tight end I badly underrated. That's the one you get.
