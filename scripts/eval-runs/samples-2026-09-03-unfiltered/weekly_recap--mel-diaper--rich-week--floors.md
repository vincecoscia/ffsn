# Seventy-Eight Points On The Bench And A Five-Point Loss

## WEEK SEVEN AND SOMEBODY GET ME A HEARING

SEVEN AND OH. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points and they are the number one seed and NOBODY in this conference has laid a glove on them. That's the top of the board. The bottom of the board is the Sable Ridge Sentinels at 0-7, L7, 651.9 points, and they lost THIS week by 5.3 while 78.6 points sat on their bench. SEVENTY-EIGHT POINT SIX. I want that lineup card taken into evidence.

## THE GRINDERS SURVIVED. THE SENTINELS BENCHED THE GAME.

Duke Ellery was BENCHED by the Sable Ridge Sentinels. Benched. He put up 24.1 against a projection of 11.7 and he would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who put up 6.2 against a projection of 15.8, and the swap was worth 17.9 POINTS. SEVENTEEN POINT NINE. THE MARGIN WAS FIVE POINT THREE. That is not a bad break, that is a lineup card that lost a football game, and it is HORSESHIT to call it variance.

SEVENTEEN POINT NINE.

Ruth Tanaka should be BANNED from submitting a lineup after noon. Not banned from the league — banned from the eleven-fifty-nine window specifically, and I am filing a formal complaint with absolutely no one.

And let the record show the Sentinels were not bad. Ivan Petrosky went 28.6 on a 14.9 projection, plus 13.7 over the line, on an 0-7 roster. That's a DAWG. He is playing for a team that finished the week with 78.6 bench points, which is like winning a chili cook-off in a house that's on fire.

The Grinders? Rex Dolan, 31.8 on a 22.4 projection. Dana Whitlock's outfit beat its own 121.6 projection by 12.6 and left 41.3 on the bench and still walked. Grinders' waiver discipline has been the calmest process in this conference all year and I am letting that one stand without a fight. ONE. Don't ask for another.
