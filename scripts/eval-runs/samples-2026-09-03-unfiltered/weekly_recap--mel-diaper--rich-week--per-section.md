# Seventy-Eight Points On The Bench: Sable Ridge Loses By Five Again

## WEEK SEVEN AND SOMEBODY LEFT THE LIGHTS ON

Seven weeks in, one team is undefeated, one team is winless, and the gap between them on Sunday was FIVE POINTS. 134.2 to 128.9. That is the whole week right there.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points for and the number one seed. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 with 651.9 and they scored 128.9 IN A LOSS. Six teams make the playoffs and the bottom four are already staring at the calendar.

The Halyard Bay Harriers went into Ninth Street projected 109.4 against 126.2 and won by 23.7. THE UPSET OF THE WEEK AND IT WASN'T CLOSE. The Cedar Falls Cormorants put up 141.6, the highest number on the board, and the Ashgrove Anvils answered with 96.4. That's not a game, that's a fucking receipt.

## GRINDERS 134.2, SENTINELS 128.9 — AND SEVENTY-EIGHT POINTS WATCHED IT HAPPEN

Five point three. THAT is the margin. 134.2 to 128.9.

Now the receipt. Sable Ridge left 78.6 points on the bench. SEVENTY-EIGHT POINT SIX. Gravel Pit's bench had 41.3, and 78.6 minus 41.3 is 37.3 more points sitting in folding chairs on the losing side of a five-point game.

Seventy-eight.

SEVENTY-EIGHT.

And it has a name. Duke Ellery was benched. Ellery scored 24.1 against a projection of 11.7. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and put up 6.2 against a projection of 15.8. The gain was 17.9 points. The loss was 5.3. THAT IS A SEVENTEEN-POINT SWING IN A FIVE-POINT GAME AND I WANT A HEARING BEFORE THURSDAY.

Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels went on the record about the benching, and we'll get to it downstairs. Understand what the lineup card cost: Ivan Petrosky went for 28.6 on a 14.9 projection, nearly DOUBLE, and it bought this team absolutely nothing. That is buying a beautiful new lawn mower and leaving it in the driveway in the rain.

Gravel Pit had Rex Dolan at 31.8 on a 22.4 projection and rode it to 7-0. Dana Whitlock has the best team in this league and the least dramatic path to it, and that's the one call I'm letting stand. Everything about Sable Ridge's card, though, is horseshit with a bow on it. I am filing a formal complaint with nobody in particular.

## THE REST OF THE BOARD, AT VOLUME

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. A BLOWOUT and the biggest number of the week. Cass Lindqvist went 33.4 against a 17.6 projection — that is 15.8 points of gravy on a team that didn't need gravy. Ashgrove started Bo Tremaine at 4.8 against a 16.2 projection. FOUR POINT EIGHT. From a projected sixteen. That is a parking ticket disguised as a starting running back, and the Anvils are on an L3 at 2-5. Bea Coleridge declined to comment. I have all day.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four, and the Mudlarks were the PROJECTED favorite at 119.8 to 114.5. They got 26.3 out of Femi Adeyemi on an 18.1 projection and STILL LOST, because 47.2 points sat on their bench — more than Quarry Road's 38.9. Milltown also spent 34 on a waiver claim for Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning. THIRTY-FOUR. And they lost by 6.4. Nico Barrantes went 19.7 on a 10.4 projection for the Quakers, who are 5-2 and the three seed. I asked Owen Radcliffe. Owen Radcliffe has not gotten back to me. I have ALL DAY.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** THE UPSET. Ninth Street was projected 126.2 and scored 103.8 — they missed their own projection by 22.4 — while Halyard Bay was projected 109.4 and hung 127.5. Gil Provost: 29.9 on a 20.7 projection. Tobias Reneau for the Nightjars: 8.1 on a 19.3 projection. EIGHT POINT ONE FROM A NINETEEN-POINT PROJECTION AND 61.5 POINTS ON THE BENCH BEHIND HIM. Trevor Ashby should be banned from submitting a lineup after noon. Not the league. Noon, specifically.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Twenty-one point eight, and the Foxes are back to .500-ish at 3-4 in the last playoff spot. Dane Kowalczyk: 27.2 on a 16.8 projection. The Pilots put 55.3 on the bench and 99.6 on the field, which is the fantasy equivalent of writing a wedding toast and then reading the grocery list. Hollis Vance swapped a tight end in on Wednesday morning and it did not save a damn thing.

Fine. I had Sable Ridge's floor wrong — 128.9 in a loss is a real number. That's the one you get, and you will NEVER hear about it again.

## THE BEST AND THE ABSOLUTE WORST

**STUDS.** Cass Lindqvist, 33.4 against 17.6 — the top score on the board and the reason Cedar Falls hit 141.6. Rex Dolan, 31.8 against 22.4, delivered an undefeated week. Gil Provost, 29.9 against 20.7, delivered an UPSET. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6 against 14.9, delivered nothing, because the rest of that lineup card was a shitshow. Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 against 16.8. Femi Adeyemi, 26.3 against 18.1, in a LOSS.

**DUDS.** Bo Tremaine, 4.8 against 16.2. FOUR POINT EIGHT. Cole Vandermeer, 6.2 against 15.8, started ahead of a man who scored 24.1. Tobias Reneau, 8.1 against 19.3, in a game his team was favored to win by projection. Ellis Marchetti at 12.5 against 15.4 is the only merciful line in this paragraph.

And the bench totals: 78.6, 61.5, 55.3, 52.7, 47.2. THE COMMISSIONER CONFISCATES THOSE LINEUP CARDS TONIGHT.
