# Gravel Pit Grinders Survive by Five, Stay Perfect at 7-0

## Week 7: Scoreboard's Open, Let's Read It

Seven weeks, seven wins. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points for, the most in this league, and Sunday they won by 5.3. Ugly? I don't care. Winning ugly is still winning, and the standings don't have an aesthetics column.

Behind them, the Cedar Falls Cormorants (5-2) dropped 141.6, the highest number on the board. The Quarry Road Quakers survived by 6.4. The Halyard Bay Harriers ran the only upset of the week. And the Sable Ridge Sentinels lost their seventh straight while scoring 128.9 — the second-highest total of the entire week. That is a fascinating way to be 0-7.

Five games. Two decided by single digits. Let's go.

## The Other Four: One Blowout, One Upset, One Six-Point Knife Fight

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. That's not a game, that's a receipt. Cass Lindqvist put up 33.4 on 17.6 projected — SHUT THE FUCK UP, that's a fucking DAWG — and Cedar Falls is 5-2 with 788.9 points for, second in the league. I called Cedar Falls a top-two points-for team back in Week 4 and the number keeps agreeing with me. Ashgrove is 2-5 and on a three-game slide, and Bo Tremaine going 4.8 on 16.2 projected is the worst starter line on the board. Bea Coleridge declined to comment. Fine. 96.4 said plenty.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four. Nico Barrantes, 19.7 from the tight end spot on a 10.4 projection — nearly double the paper, and the paper is a bedtime story. Quarry Road is 5-2 and third. Now here's the part that stings: Femi Adeyemi went for 26.3 for Milltown and it was not enough. The Mudlarks also spent 34 on a waiver claim for Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning. Owen Radcliffe didn't talk to Sam. The scoreboard did: 112.3, and 3-4.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** UPSET OF THE WEEK. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and scored 127.5. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and scored 103.8. Projections are horseshit and the Harriers just proved it by 23.7 while everybody's grade card had it the other way. Gil Provost, 29.9. Two straight wins, 4-3. Meanwhile Ninth Street started Tobias Reneau for 8.1 against a 19.3 projection and had 61.5 sitting on the bench, 27.7 more than Halyard Bay's 33.8. Should-have is not a column in the standings.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 on 16.8 projected, and Foundry District is off the mat at 3-4 and holding the last playoff seed. Pike County scored 99.6, benched 55.3, and is 2-5 on a two-game skid. Ellis Marchetti at 12.5 was their best starter on the board. That's not enough anywhere.
