# Gravel Pit Grinders Survive by 5.3 as Sable Ridge Leaves 78.6 on the Bench

## WEEK SEVEN AND SOMEBODY BENCHED 78.6 POINTS

SEVEN WEEKS IN AND A TEAM SAT DOWN 78.6 POINTS. Seventy-eight point six! On a bench! In a game they lost by 5.3! That is the Sable Ridge Sentinels, 0-7, seven straight losses, and I have the box score in my hand and it is STILL WARM.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points and they are the only unbeaten thing left in this conference. Everybody else is scrapping over the six playoff seats, and the whole damn week came down to margins: 5.3, 6.4, and then a 45.2-point blowout that looked like somebody left the parking brake on.

TWO THINGS I WANT ON THE RECORD. One: there should be a HEARING about a bench that outscored nothing and cost everything. Two: the Sentinels' lineup card should be reviewed by a panel of strangers before week eight, because 0-7 with 651.9 points is not bad luck, it is a plan that went to hell.

WHAT THE HELL WAS THE PLAN?!

## THE GRINDERS WIN BY 5.3 AND I AM STILL SHOUTING ABOUT THE BENCH

134.2 to 128.9. The Gravel Pit Grinders were projected 121.6 and beat it. Rex Dolan went for 31.8 on a 22.4 projection — that is 9.4 over the number and that is the whole damn game, because the margin was 5.3.

Now the receipt that ends careers. The Sable Ridge Sentinels started Cole Vandermeer, who was projected 15.8 and scored 6.2. Duke Ellery was BENCHED. Ellery scored 24.1 against an 11.7 projection. Swap him in for Vandermeer and that is 17.9 points back.

SEVENTEEN POINT NINE. IN A GAME DECIDED BY 5.3.

Ruth Tanaka went on the record about it, and we will get to that, because it is the worst piece of paper on this desk.

Ivan Petrosky did everything a running back can do — 28.6 on a 14.9 projection, nearly double the number — and the Sentinels are still 0-7. That is a half-assed lineup card wasting a monster afternoon, and I am filing a formal complaint with nobody in particular.

TWO DEMANDS. One: Ruth Tanaka should be BANNED from touching the flex slot for the rest of October. Not the league. The flex slot, specifically. Two: the desk wants the Grinders' 41.3 bench points reviewed too, because winning by 5.3 with 41.3 sitting down is not a plan, it is a parking ticket you got lucky enough to have voided.

Dana Whitlock's Grinders have the discipline. They also have 7-0 and a 5.3-point margin and one of those numbers is lying to you.?!

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four. From believing them.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 on a 16.8 projection, and the Foxes are back to the last playoff seat at 3-4. Hollis Vance is running a lineup card like a lawn mower with no blade — plenty of noise, nothing cut.
