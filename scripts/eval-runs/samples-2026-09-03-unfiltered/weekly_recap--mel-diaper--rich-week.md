# Seventy-Eight Points of Bench: Sable Ridge Loses by Five

## WEEK SEVEN AND SOMEBODY CALL A HEARING

SEVEN AND OH. That is the Gravel Pit Grinders through seven weeks, seven straight wins, 812.4 points for, and they got there this week by FIVE POINT THREE. FIVE POINT THREE!

Week seven gave us one nail-biter, one blowout, one upset and a lineup card that should be entered into evidence. The Cedar Falls Cormorants put 141.6 on the board — highest number of the week — and won by 45.2. The Halyard Bay Harriers were projected 109.4 and scored 127.5 and mugged the Ninth Street Nightjars. And the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7, seven losses, SEVEN, with 78.6 points sitting on their bench.

Six teams make the playoffs in this league. The line right now sits at 3-4, which means the Foundry District Foxes are in and the Milltown Mudlarks are out on 705.2 to 720.7 points for. FIFTEEN POINT FIVE POINTS is the whole difference between a seat and a shrug, and I want a hearing about it.

I ALSO WANT THE ANVILS BANNED FROM THE RUNNING BACK POSITION. Not the league. The position. The receipt is coming.

## GRINDERS 134.2, SENTINELS 128.9 — AND THE BENCH DID IT

Five point three. FIVE POINT THREE. That is what separated the Gravel Pit Grinders from the Sable Ridge Sentinels, and I want you looking at the bench line while I say it.

The Sentinels bench scored 78.6. The Grinders bench scored 41.3. That is 37.3 points of difference and it was ALL ON THE WRONG SIDE OF THE LINEUP CARD.

Here's the receipt, and it's the worst one on the board. Duke Ellery was BENCHED. Cole Vandermeer started in his place and put up 6.2 against a 15.8 projection. Ellery went for 24.1. Swap them and Sable Ridge gains 17.9 points. THE MARGIN WAS 5.3. Seventeen point nine into a five point three hole. I am filing a formal complaint with absolutely no one.

SEVENTEEN POINT NINE. FIVE POINT THREE. THAT LINEUP CARD SHOULD BE CONFISCATED AND FRAMED IN A HALLWAY AS A WARNING.

Ruth Tanaka told Sam in week six that the desk should try setting a lineup once. THE DESK SHOULD TRY SETTING A LINEUP ONCE?! I called the Sentinels lineup the least defensible in the league in week five and the follow-up is a 24.1-point receiver in a windbreaker.

Rex Dolan of the Grinders did his job — 31.8 against a 22.4 projection, nine point four over. Ivan Petrosky of the Sentinels went 28.6 on a 14.9 projection and it bought him NOTHING. Dana Whitlock's Grinders are 7-0 and I will say this once: the Grinders did not need a fluke, they needed a 12.6-point beat on their own 121.6 projection, and they got it.
