# Gravel Pit Grinders Stay Perfect by Five Points

## Good Evening

Good evening. The only unbeaten team in this conference beat the only winless one by 5.3 points, and that is the headline of week seven.

The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7. Both of those records survived a game that came down to less than the value of one long catch.

Elsewhere: one blowout, one upset, and two games decided by more than 20. The top six seeds are set for now, and only two games separate the second seed from the sixth.

Let's go to the board.

## Game of the Week: Grinders 134.2, Sentinels 128.9

Tightest game on the board: Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Margin, 5.3. Everything else on the slate was decided by more than that, which is the polite way to put it.

Rex Dolan went for 31.8 for the Grinders against a projection of 22.4. That is a score.

Sable Ridge got 28.6 from Ivan Petrosky on a 14.9 projection and still lost. The reason is on the bench. Duke Ellery was benched for the Sentinels and scored 24.1; he would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and put up 6.2, for a gain of 17.9 points. The final margin was 5.3. Do with that what you will.

Sable Ridge also outscored the Grinders on the bench, 78.6 to 41.3, a difference of 37.3. Nina has the bench math.

Dana Whitlock's Grinders are now on a seven-game winning streak and lead the conference at 812.4 points for. That is the extra beat this result has earned all season: nobody has scored more, and nobody has beaten them.

We'll have more on that after the break.

## Around the Board

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Highest score of the week, 141.6, and the widest margin, 45.2. Cass Lindqvist put up 33.4 for the Cormorants against a 17.6 projection — the top individual line on the board. Ashgrove started Bo Tremaine for 4.8 on a 16.2 projection and has now lost three straight. We reached out to the Ashgrove Anvils and did not hear back. Noted.

Marcus Bly's Cormorants also worked the wire, claiming Trey Ballinger for 12 dollars. Dex is working the phones on what that roster wants next.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Second-closest game of the week at 6.4 points, and the Mudlarks were the projected side, 119.8 to 114.5. Nico Barrantes went 19.7 at tight end for the Quakers on a 10.4 projection. Femi Adeyemi led all Milltown starters with 26.3 and it wasn't enough. Priya Nandi's Quakers are 5-2 and hold the third seed.

Milltown spent 34 dollars on the wire for Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday morning, the biggest claim of the week. We reached out to the Milltown Mudlarks and did not hear back. Noted.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** The upset of the week, by 23.7. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and finished 103.8, a shortfall of 22.4. Gil Provost went 29.9 for the Harriers. Tobias Reneau went 8.1 for the Nightjars on a 19.3 projection, and Trevor Ashby's team left 61.5 on the bench against Halyard Bay's 33.8. Second straight win for the Harriers, who now sit fifth at 4-3. We'll leave that there.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Comfortable, 21.8. Dane Kowalczyk carried the Foxes with 27.2 on a 16.8 projection. Ellis Marchetti was the best Pike County starter at 12.5, under his 15.4 projection, and the Pilots' bench outscored the Foxes' bench 55.3 to 36.4. Sam Kestrel's Foxes hold the sixth and final playoff seed at 3-4; Hollis Vance's Pilots are 2-5 and have lost two.

Coming up: the best and the worst of it.

## Studs and Duds

Studs, in order. Cass Lindqvist, 33.4 for Cedar Falls, top score on the board. Rex Dolan, 31.8 for the Grinders in a game decided by 5.3. Gil Provost, 29.9 in the upset. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6 for Sable Ridge, and no win to show for it. Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2. Femi Adeyemi, 26.3 in a 6.4-point loss.
