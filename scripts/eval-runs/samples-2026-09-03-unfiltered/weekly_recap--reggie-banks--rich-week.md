# Gravel Pit Grinders Go 7-0 and the Scoreboard Doesn't Care About Your Projection

## Week 7: Somebody Is Still Undefeated

Seven games, seven wins, 812.4 points for. The Gravel Pit Grinders are the only unbeaten team left in this conference and they got there this week by five and change over a team that has not won a game all year. Dana Whitlock's Grinders have won every single one, and the streak is now seven long.

Behind them: Cedar Falls hung the biggest number of the week, Halyard Bay stole the game nobody had them stealing, and the projection sheet went 0-for-the-afternoon in the two games that mattered most. Five games, three teams beat their projection, and every one of them won.

And at the bottom, the Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7 — seven losses in a row — after coming within 5.3 of the best team in the league. That is the week. Let's go.

## Grinders 134.2, Sentinels 128.9 — Five Points and a Bench Full of Receipts

134.2 to 128.9. Margin: 5.3. Nail-biter of the week and the game that keeps the perfect record perfect.

Rex Dolan, quarterback, 31.8 points on a 22.4 projection. That's 9.4 more than the paper said, in a game decided by 5.3. Do that arithmetic however you like — he WAS the margin. GIVE THAT MAN HIS FLOWERS. The Grinders were projected for 121.6 and scored 134.2. Projections are a bedtime story. Dana Whitlock's team is 7-0 and leads the league in points for at 812.4, and I don't care where anybody on that roster was taken.

Now the other sideline, and I'm going to say this the way I'd say it to a teammate. The Sable Ridge Sentinels scored 128.9. That is a good number! That beats their 118.3 projection by more than ten. Ivan Petrosky went 28.6 on a 14.9 line — the man is 0-7 and still running downhill, and I mean it when I say put some respect on that.

But Cole Vandermeer started and scored 6.2 against a 15.8 projection, and Duke Ellery sat on the bench and scored 24.1. Swap those two and Sable Ridge gains 17.9 points. They lost by 5.3. There were 78.6 points sitting on that bench in total — the most of anybody on the board, winners included.

Ruth Tanaka went on the record about it, and you'll hear from her later in this column. I'll give her this: she named the number herself. Nobody had to tell her.

Zero and seven. I'll wait.

## The Rest of the Board

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. That's the biggest number anybody hung all week and the biggest margin. Cass Lindqvist went for 33.4 on a 17.6 projection — nearly double the bedtime story, and the Cormorants are 5-2 with 788.9 points for, second in the league. Marcus Bly also claimed Trey Ballinger off waivers this week for twelve bucks. Twelve. That's how a 5-2 team shops. On the other side, Bo Tremaine started and scored 4.8 against a 16.2 projection for the Ashgrove Anvils. Three straight losses, 2-5. Bea Coleridge declined to comment. The scoreboard talked: 96.4.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four, and the Mudlarks were the projected side at 119.8 to 114.5. Paper doesn't score. Nico Barrantes went 19.7 from tight end on a 10.4 projection for the Quakers — Priya Nandi's team is 5-2 and third and won a game they were supposed to lose. Femi Adeyemi gave the Mudlarks 26.3 and it wasn't enough. Owen Radcliffe didn't talk to Sam. The scoreboard did: 112.3, 3-4, and out of the top six by one game. He also spent $34 on Rudy Halvorsen Wednesday morning. I like the aggression. Show me the points.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8. UPSET OF THE WEEK.** The Nightjars were projected for 126.2. They scored 103.8. The Harriers were projected for 109.4. They scored 127.5 and won by 23.7. That projection sheet is horseshit and I say that with a smile. Gil Provost put up 29.9 on a 20.7 line. Lena Okafor's team is 4-3 and riding a two-game streak. Trevor Ashby's Nightjars got 8.1 out of Tobias Reneau on a 19.3 projection and left 61.5 on the bench — the most bench production of anybody who lost. Should-have is not a column in the standings.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Twenty-one point eight. Dane Kowalczyk ran for 27.2 on a 16.8 projection and Sam Kestrel's Foxes are back in the sixth seed at 3-4. That's the playoff line, held. The Pike County Pilots are 2-5 on an L2 with 664.1 points for, ninth in the league. Hollis Vance swapped in Nate Brill on Wednesday. The Pilots put up 99.6. Next.
