# Gravel Pit Grinders Go 7-0 By Five Points And I'm Not Taking Questions

## Week 7: The Scoreboard Is Still Undefeated

Seven weeks in, one team has not lost a football game. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points for, the most in this league, and they got there Sunday by five and a third points. SEVEN AND OH. Behind them it's the Cedar Falls Cormorants at 5-2 with 788.9, the Quarry Road Quakers at 5-2, and then a knife fight for the last playoff seats — six teams make it, and the Foundry District Foxes are sitting sixth at 3-4 by the skin of a win.

One upset on the board, one blowout, two one-possession finishes, and a bench that cost somebody a football game. Let's read the box scores out loud.

## The Other Four: A Blowout, A Robbery, And Two Comfortable Ones

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. That is not a game, that is a receipt. Cass Lindqvist put 33.4 on the board for Cedar Falls against a 17.6 projection — 15.8 points more than the paper said, and the paper can eat it. On the other side Bo Tremaine started for the Ashgrove Anvils and returned 4.8 points on a 16.2 projection. Somebody had to take that one. Bea Coleridge declined to talk to Sam. The scoreboard talked: 2-5, three straight losses. Cedar Falls is 5-2 and second in points for at 788.9 — I called them a top-two points-for team back in Week 4 and that one hit.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four. The Mudlarks were the projected side at 119.8 and lost the game anyway, which is how I know projections are horseshit. Femi Adeyemi went for 26.3 for Milltown and it wasn't enough, because Priya Nandi's Quakers got 19.7 from Nico Barrantes at tight end on a 10.4 projection. A tight end doubling his number is how you win by a field goal. Milltown also spent 34 on Rudy Halvorsen off waivers Wednesday morning — I like the aggression, I need the points. Owen Radcliffe didn't talk to Sam. The scoreboard did: 3-4, and third in the standings from the wrong end of the playoff line.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** UPSET, and not a close one — 23.7. Ninth Street was projected 126.2 and scored 103.8. Halyard Bay was projected 109.4 and scored 127.5. Meanwhile Trevor Ashby's Nightjars left 61.5 on the bench and started Tobias Reneau for 8.1 against a 19.3 projection. Should-have is not a column in the standings. GIVE HALYARD BAY THEIR FLOWERS — they were the underdog and they won by damn near four touchdowns.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 for the Foxes on a 16.8 projection. Sam Kestrel's Foxes are 3-4 and holding the last playoff seat, and they got there by 21.8. Pike County scored 99.6 with 55.3 on the bench — more points watching than most teams got playing. Ellis Marchetti's 12.5 was the best thing they started. 2-5, two straight losses. I'll wait.
