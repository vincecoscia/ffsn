# Gravel Pit Grinders Go 7-0 by Five Points and I'm Still Screaming

## SCOREBOARD DAY, BABY

Seven weeks in and the Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0 with 812.4 points. That's not a hot start, that's a résumé.

Week 7 gave us a five-point escape at the top, the week's biggest number out of the Cedar Falls Cormorants at 141.6, and the Halyard Bay Harriers walking into Ninth Street projected to lose and winning by 23.7. Projections are a bedtime story! The playoff field is six deep, and right now the sixth seed is the Foundry District Foxes at 3-4 — one game up on nobody, because the Milltown Mudlarks are 3-4 too and sitting seventh on points. Six teams get in. Four are already fighting for two chairs. Buckle up.

## GAME OF THE WEEK: FIVE POINTS AND A PERFECT RECORD

Gravel Pit Grinders 134.2, Sable Ridge Sentinels 128.9. Margin: 5.3. The Grinders were projected for 121.6 and put up 134.2. THAT is how an undefeated team stays undefeated — you beat your own paper by double digits when the game is tight.

Rex Dolan, 31.8 points, in the lineup, projected for 22.4. I don't care where Dana Whitlock took him. He played, he scored, the Grinders are 7-0. GIVE THAT MAN HIS FLOWERS.

And look — 128.9 is a real number. That's more than the Harriers, the Foxes and the Quakers all scored this week. The Sentinels are not scoring like a 0-7 team. They're losing like one, and the difference between those two sentences is a lineup card. Set it and this column gets a lot nicer.

## THE REST OF THE LEAGUE, RESULTS FIRST

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. That's the week's high score and it wasn't close for a second. Cass Lindqvist went 33.4 with 17.6 on the projection sheet — nearly doubled the paper. Marcus Bly also went and claimed Trey Ballinger for 12 bucks on Wednesday morning while everyone else was reading grade cards. Cedar Falls: 5-2, 788.9 points, second seed. My Week 4 take was that Cedar Falls finishes top two in points for. Still on track. Scoreboard.

On the other side, Bo Tremaine started and scored 4.8 against a 16.2 projection. Ashgrove is 2-5 on a three-game skid. Bea Coleridge declined to comment. The scoreboard commented plenty.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** Six point four. The Mudlarks were the projected side at 119.8 and came up 112.3 — should-have is not a column in the standings. Femi Adeyemi went off for 26.3 and it wasn't enough, which is the cruelest way to lose. Owen Radcliffe didn't talk to Sam. The scoreboard did: 3-4, seventh seed, out of the six.

Give Priya Nandi her flowers, though. Nico Barrantes, a tight end, 19.7 points on a 10.4 projection, and that's your margin. She also went and got Juno Castellanos and Vic Trawick from the Foxes for Hank Osterberg back in Week 6. Quarry Road is 5-2 and third. That trade worked. Say it out loud.

And the Mudlarks spent 34 on Rudy Halvorsen Wednesday morning. I like the aggression. I like it more when it shows up in the win column.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** UPSET OF THE WEEK AND IT WASN'T EVEN A NAIL-BITER. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and hung 127.5. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2 and managed 103.8. That's a 23.7-point beatdown from the team nobody picked. Gil Provost: 29.9 points, projected 20.7. Lena Okafor started him and he delivered. THAT'S A DAWG.

For Trevor Ashby: Tobias Reneau started, scored 8.1 against a 19.3 projection, and the Nightjars had 61.5 points sitting on the bench. I'm not going to pretend that's fun to look at. Ninth Street is 4-3 and still the fourth seed. Fix the lineup card, keep the seed.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 on a 16.8 projection, and Sam Kestrel's Foxes clear the paper again — projected 115.1, scored 121.4. That's the sixth seed, held by force. The Pilots got 12.5 out of Ellis Marchetti, fell to 2-5, and are last in points for among the living at 664.1. Hollis Vance added Nate Brill Wednesday morning. Keep swinging.

## FLOWERS AND FRIDGE MAGNETS

**THE DAWGS.**

Cass Lindqvist, 33.4. Highest individual number of the week, on the highest team score of the week, 141.6. Put some respect on it.

Rex Dolan, 31.8, in a game his team won by 5.3. Nine and change over his projection of 22.4 (31.8 minus 22.4). Undefeated teams have guys like that.

Gil Provost, 29.9 in an upset win. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6 for the Sentinels in a loss — 28.6 on a 14.9 projection is a monster game and it deserves better than 0-7. Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2. Femi Adeyemi, 26.3 in a loss by 6.4. Nico Barrantes, 19.7 from the tight end spot, which won a football game.

**THE COLD ONES.**

Bo Tremaine: 4.8 started, 16.2 projected. Cole Vandermeer: 6.2 started, 15.8 projected, and his 24.1-point teammate watched from the bench. Tobias Reneau: 8.1 started, 19.3 projected, in a 23.7-point loss.

Four starters under nine points across this league this week. Three of them were on losing teams. That's not bad luck, that's the scoreboard doing bookkeeping.

## THEY SPOKE, I ANSWERED

Dana Whitlock of the Gravel Pit Grinders got asked about starting 7-0, and gave us the most professional answer of the season. The Cormorants come to town in Week 8, 5-2 against 7-0, and that is the game of the year so far.

Ruth Tanaka of the Sable Ridge Sentinels got asked about the 24.1 points in street clothes, and answered it twice. Credit for standing in there. The number is still the number.

Lena Okafor of the Halyard Bay Harriers got asked about the upset, and I want that sentence stitched on a banner. 127.5 to 103.8. Projections are bedtime stories and Halyard Bay just woke up. THAT'S A DAWG.

Here's my Week 8 call: the Grinders beat the Cormorants and go 8-0. If Cedar Falls outscores them, I was wrong and I'll say so loud. You can take that to the bank.
