# Gravel Pit Grinders Go 7-0 By Five Points, And The Scoreboard Doesn't Care How

## Seven And Oh And Still Standing

Week 7 in the Ironclad Fantasy Conference and there is still a zero in the loss column at the top of the board. The Gravel Pit Grinders are 7-0, seven straight wins, 812.4 points for, and they had to survive a 5.3-point nail-biter to keep it. That's the story of the week: the best team in the league won ugly and won anyway.

Six teams make the playoffs and the top six is already starting to look like a club — the Grinders at 7-0, Cedar Falls at 5-2, Quarry Road at 5-2, then Ninth Street and Halyard Bay at 4-3 and the Foundry District Foxes at 3-4. The Sable Ridge Sentinels are 0-7. Seven weeks, seven losses.

Five games, three of them decided by the paper being wrong. Let's go to the scoreboard, because it's the only thing in this building not lying to you.

## Grinders 134.2, Sentinels 128.9 — Five Points And A Bench Full Of Receipts

Five point three. That's the margin. The Gravel Pit Grinders were projected for 121.6 and put up 134.2, because Rex Dolan went out and hung 31.8 on a 22.4 projection. Dana Whitlock started him, he scored, and I don't care where he was taken. GIVE THAT MAN HIS FLOWERS.

Now the other side, and I'm going to say this plain because it's the biggest receipt on the board. Sable Ridge scored 128.9 and had 78.6 points sitting on the bench — 37.3 more bench production than the Grinders' 41.3. Duke Ellery was benched. He scored 24.1. He would have replaced Cole Vandermeer, who started and scored 6.2. That's a 17.9-point gain, and the Sentinels lost by 5.3.

**Cedar Falls Cormorants 141.6, Ashgrove Anvils 96.4.** Forty-five point two. That is not a game, that is a fucking statement. Cass Lindqvist: 33.4 points on a 17.6 projection. On the other side, Bo Tremaine started and gave the Anvils 4.8 against a 16.2 projection, and Ashgrove is 2-5 on an L3. Bea Coleridge declined to talk to Sam. The scoreboard talked: 96.4.

**Quarry Road Quakers 118.7, Milltown Mudlarks 112.3.** The Mudlarks were the ones projected to win this — 119.8 to 114.5 — and the paper is a bedtime story. Femi Adeyemi went for 26.3 for Milltown and it still wasn't enough, because Nico Barrantes put 19.7 on a 10.4 projection at tight end for Priya Nandi's Quakers. Margin: 6.4. Quarry Road is 5-2 and third. Milltown claimed Rudy Halvorsen on Wednesday for $34 and lost by 6.4 — Owen Radcliffe didn't talk to Sam. The scoreboard did. 112.3.

**Halyard Bay Harriers 127.5, Ninth Street Nightjars 103.8.** UPSET OF THE WEEK. Ninth Street was projected for 126.2. They scored 103.8. Halyard Bay was projected for 109.4 and dropped 127.5 on them. Twenty-three point seven. Gil Provost: 29.9 points, 20.7 projected. Lena Okafor's team is 4-3 and riding two straight. Meanwhile Trevor Ashby started Tobias Reneau for 8.1 against a 19.3 projection and left 61.5 on the bench to Halyard Bay's 33.8. Should-have is not a column in the standings.

**Foundry District Foxes 121.4, Pike County Pilots 99.6.** Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2 points on a 16.8 projection, and Sam Kestrel's team wins by 21.8 to get back to 3-4 and into the sixth seed. Pike County got 12.5 from Ellis Marchetti, left 55.3 on the bench, and is 2-5 with an L2. That's the whole game.

## Flowers And Frowns

**THE FLOWERS.** Cass Lindqvist, 33.4 in a 45.2-point win. That's a DAWG. Rex Dolan, 31.8 in a game the Gravel Pit Grinders won by 5.3 — that man is the reason there's still a zero in that loss column. Gil Provost, 29.9 in the upset. Ivan Petrosky, 28.6 for a team that's 0-7, which is the loneliest great game in the conference. Dane Kowalczyk, 27.2, and Femi Adeyemi, 26.3, both over their numbers.

**THE FROWNS.** Bo Tremaine started for Ashgrove and scored 4.8 on a 16.2 projection. Cole Vandermeer started for Sable Ridge and scored 6.2 on 15.8 — with 24.1 sitting behind him on the bench. Tobias Reneau, 8.1 on 19.3, in a game Ninth Street lost by 23.7.
